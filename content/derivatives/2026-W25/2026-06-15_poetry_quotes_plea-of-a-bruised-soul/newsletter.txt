Subject: Don't fix me. Just stay.
Preview: A poem about what a bruised soul actually needs from you.

This week I published a poem that came from a very specific place.

Not from pain itself — but from the loneliness of having your pain redirected. You know the feeling. Someone who loves you hears what you're carrying and immediately offers a reframe, a silver lining, a lesson. They mean well. But you walk away feeling more alone than before you said anything.

The poem is called "Plea of a Bruised Soul."

It's a plea not for rescue — but for witness. For someone to sit in the room where the ceiling has caved and not look away.

I also wrote two reflections alongside it: one about a moment when someone's well-meant comfort left me feeling unseen, and one about a time I was the one doing the over-advising. Both were harder to write than the poem.

If you've ever needed someone to just stay — this one is for you.
