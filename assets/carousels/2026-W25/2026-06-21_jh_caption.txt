I automated the worst part of job hunting. 🤖

Most job search advice is "apply to more jobs."

Nobody tells you how to actually *find* the right ones without spending 3 hours a day on career pages.

So I built a script.

Every night it:
→ Scrapes 130+ company career pages for new roles
→ Scores every role 0–100 against my resume using an LLM
→ Texts me only my top matches on Telegram

By morning, I have a curated shortlist. No LinkedIn doomscrolling. No missed opportunities at companies I actually care about.

Free. Open source. Runs on your own machine. No subscription. No data leaving your system.
