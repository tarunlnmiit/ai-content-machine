BREATH OF POETRY

When Dreams
Speak of Love

on loving someone safe — and alive

"in the dark, wrapped in a blanket that feels like the whole world"

THE QUESTION SHIFTS

Are they good enough?

Am I brave enough to let them in?

THE POEM

"safe, but alive"

"

the sweetest symphony entered my ears

"the twinkle in your eyes can rival that of a star"

"dissolving in my arms, out of sheer love and embarrassment"

MOST LOVE SONGS COLLAPSE
SAFE becomes settling
ALIVE becomes chaos
THE THESIS

This poem refuses to choose.

They hold you steady while you expand

nervous system relaxes + aliveness opens up
the poem gives you texture —
darkness blanket bonfire mama

not symbols — actual places where you stop performing and just exist

THE TURN

What most love stories get wrong

They melt because their beloved lights up talking about someone else.

that's a different ache entirely

THE ACHE OF INTEGRATION

the circles of your life collapse into one

not as a guest — as someone who was always supposed to be there

chosen by someone your whole world would also choose

Not triumph. Not arrival. Dissolution.
THE EMBARRASSMENT IS THE KEY

the vulnerability of wanting something so badly you can already feel its texture

already taste it would risk everything for it

a dream almost real enough to touch

something comes loose — like a knot finally giving way

love isn't complete until you can imagine them fully alive in the lives of the people you came from

SAFE ≠ unchanged ALIVE ≠ reckless

they live in the same moment

you stop being divided. you just love them.
