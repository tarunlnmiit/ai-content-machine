# 02 — Emotional Idea Bank (poetry + life)

Concept menu for ideation. Each idea = an emotional hook archetype (`01`) + a theme that has resonated, mapped to
the arc (`00`). Because the pipeline starts from a blog, each is framed as a **blog/YouTube topic** that compresses
into clips, carousels, and reels.

Format per idea: **Hook → core feeling → the turn → permission/CTA**.

## Poetry — themes that retain (specificity + being seen)

1. **The identity split** — "a data scientist who cries at poems." The precision-vs-feeling contradiction;
   universalizes "I'm not supposed to feel this, but I do." → Hook: Contrarian Mirror.
2. **The specific loss** — not "grief" but *how* it shows up: a name said one last time, an unsent text, a chair.
   → Hook: Specific Loss. (Poetry's highest-retention lane.)
3. **Naming an unnamed feeling** — the quiet dread before a good thing ends; the loneliness inside a crowd.
   → Hook: Open Question / Redefinition.
4. **Anti-toxic-positivity permission** — "don't fix me, just stay"; sadness that doesn't need a silver lining.
   → Hook: Permission.
5. **The mask / performance** — waking up to the version of yourself you built for others.
   → Hook: Contrarian Mirror.

## Life — themes that save (mechanism + permission)

6. **Systems-failure relatability** — "I had five productivity apps and wrote nothing." The tools were the avoidance.
   → Hook: Social Proof Inversion.
7. **The boring truth** — the most consistent people don't have complicated systems. → Hook: Bold Declaration.
8. **Cost-proximity** — advice sticks only when it arrives near real cost; that's why you re-buy the same book.
   → Hook: Open Question.
9. **Sequence truth** — you can't shortcut readiness/grief by understanding it faster. → Hook: Bold Declaration.
10. **The one practice + its real cost story** — one habit, grounded in a moment it actually mattered (burnout, a
    distracted dinner). → Hook: Social Proof Inversion.

## Using the bank in a blog-first flow
- Pick an idea → it becomes the **blog angle** (use the matching archetype for the title).
- The blog's most resonant 30-45s passage becomes the **reel/clip hook + arc**.
- Life ideas (6-10) spawn a **carousel** naturally (one slide per mechanism/step) — the proven save-bait shape.
- Poetry ideas carry into the **reel** as the spoken piece with burned captions.
- Keep it honest: every "cost story" must be real (see `04_authenticity_guardrail.md`).
