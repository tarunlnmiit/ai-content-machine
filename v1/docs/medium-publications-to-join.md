## How "getting added as a writer" works on Medium (2026)

A publication appears in your **Contributing** tab once an editor adds you as a writer. The general flow:

1. **Follow** the publication.
2. Open its **"Write for us" / submission guidelines** article (usually pinned, or linked from the pub's About page).
3. Apply via the method that pub specifies — one of: a **Google Form / Typeform**, **email an editor**, or **comment your @username** under the guidelines post.
4. Editor adds you → pub shows under **Contributing** → you can submit drafts.
5. Some pubs skip step 3: if you follow them and they're "accepting submissions," you can submit a draft directly and acceptance = de facto writer status.

Tip: paste a pub's homepage URL into Medium's "Submit to a publication" search to check live accepting-status before investing effort.
