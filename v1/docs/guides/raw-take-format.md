# Raw Take — raw opinion Shorts (Life niche)

> **Language decision 2026-07-12: ALL ENGLISH** (spoken, captions, metadata) — supersedes the Hinglish spec below. Question bank now carries `q_en` fields. Recording folds into the raw-session lane (`raw-session-lane.md`); the spoken question doubles as the slicer marker.
> **Amended 2026-07-14:** English-only applies to the spoken question marker + captions/metadata. The answer itself may freely code-switch English/Hinglish — see `raw-session-lane.md`.

*Added 2026-06-20. A separate, additive content lane — does NOT replace the weekly blog / long-form / 9 reels.*

## What it is
A real (or hypothetical) question someone asked → Tarun's honest, unscripted opinion **in English** → one landing line. Posted as **IG Reel (@mistakenlyhuman) + YouTube Short (Breath of Life)**.

## Cadence
- **4×/week**, batch-recorded in one sitting. (Daily only if 2-week retention holds — check `weekly_winners.py`.)
- The week's 4 questions auto-surface in `data/ideas/weekly_ideas.md` under **Life** → the
  **🎤 Raw Take batch** block (rotated by ISO week from `data/kb/raw_take_questions.json`).

## Structure (20–45s)
1. **Hook = the question, verbatim.** "Someone asked me — `<question>`." The question is the hook; first 3s decide it.
2. **Raw opinion in English.** Personal example > advice. Analytical-but-warm. As it actually comes out — don't script it flat.
3. **Land on one line.** A reframe or permission, not a slogan. Optionally invite the next question in comments (fuels the format + DMs).

## Rules
- Burn **English captions** (40% watch sound-off) — `embedded-captions` skill, `anchor` identity.
- Banned: "In conclusion", "Dive into", "Leverage", "Game-changer", "Synergy", guru tone, motivational crescendo.
- **Keep it distinct.** One real question per short — never recycle a question or split one take into many. That's the line between this and spray.

## Close the loop
After 2 weeks, run the winners check and keep the question **themes** that hit
(relationships / career / self-doubt / discipline / comparison); drop the rest. Best source of new
questions = your real DMs and comments (authentic + on-trend for your audience).

## Why it's viable
Life + emotional + IG is the proven winner (top posts: "Time heals all wounds", habits→system).
The Q&A format is self-hooking, the unscripted rawness is the authenticity edge, and it's the
cheapest distinct-idea content to produce. Risk is volume creep — capped at 4×/week + batch to avoid it.
