### 3. What Humans Are Stories Publishes

Humans Are Stories is a home for **poetry and the human side of life**. As of June 2026, we welcome:

✅ **Poetry** — any form, free verse included (tag: `Poetry`)

✅ **Life & personal stories** — life lessons, "this happened to me," reflections, personal essays (tag: `Life`)

✅ **Self-improvement** — honest, lived advice on becoming a little better (tag: `Self Improvement`)

✅ **Mental health** — personal experiences, healing, awareness (tag: `Mental Health`)

✅ **Shortform** — short stories and flash pieces (tag: `Short Story`)

If it's about being human — what you feel, learn, survive, or notice — it belongs here.

❌ **What we don't publish:** technical or tech news, data/AI tutorials, product or industry news, marketing/SEO pieces, listicles, and clickbait. This is not a tech publication. Anything that reads like a news report or a how-to-for-machines is not a fit.
