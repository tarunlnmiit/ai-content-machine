**Channel:** 161 subscribers · 22 videos

|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Impressions CTR | **0.7%** (most recent video: **0.3%**) | 5%+ | 🔴 Critical |

| Impressions | 5,800 | — | ✅ Algorithm is trying |
| Views (28 days) | 100 | — | — |

### What the numbers say
YouTube showed the most recent video (Tutorial 1/10) to **5,700 people**. Only **17 clicked** (0.3% CTR). Of those 17, the average person left after **32 seconds** of a 16-minute video (3.3% retention). YouTube will now stop distributing this video — both signals are telling it "people don't want this."

The algorithm is not the problem. 79.7% of impressions came from YouTube recommending the content. It gave the video chances. The thumbnail and opening failed.

**Traffic mix:** External 29% · YouTube search 26% · Suggested 21% · Browse 6%

The best video ever ("Data Preprocessing," 6.9k views, 3 years ago) came from search, not browse — a one-time wave that didn't compound.

### Issues

**1. Thumbnail has no face — this is the CTR killer**
Every visible DS thumbnail uses dark background + text diagrams. No face. The most recent video uses a "4-Pillar Framework" diagram with small labels. At 0.3% CTR, this thumbnail is invisible.
Fix: Your face (confused/surprised expression) + 3–4 words of hook text, high contrast. The face isn't vanity — it's the signal that makes humans stop scrolling.

**2. Title is descriptive, not a hook**
"Python for Data Science Beginners 2026 — Stop Setting Up Wrong (Tutorial 1/10)"
- "Stop Setting Up Wrong" is too vague. What goes wrong specifically? What's the cost?
- "(Tutorial 1/10)" actively reduces clicks — commitment aversion. Viewers don't want to sign up for a series before they've seen value. Remove it or move it to the description.
- Better formula: `[Specific mistake] → [Specific consequence]` e.g. "The Python Setup Mistake That Breaks Your First Project"

**3. Opening 30 seconds is failing**
AVD of 0:32 means people clicked and left almost immediately. The opening is almost certainly: title card → intro → "in this video I'm going to show you." Start with the specific problem or mistake in the first sentence.

**4. Copyright flags on 3 videos**
"Pandas Series Part 1 & 2" and "Pandas Indexing" all have Copyright restrictions. Check if these are limiting distribution.

### What's working
- **Shorts are the healthiest asset.** "Why Your Code Breaks in Docker" (32 views), "The First Skill Python Tutorials Skip" (27 views), "Reading Python Errors" (21 views) — all outperforming the long-form video. The Shorts hook is landing.
- The channel got **+9 subscribers** in 28 days, almost entirely from Shorts.

**1. Multiple videos use "Breath of Poetry & Life" branded thumbnails — no face**
I saw at least 3 videos with a text-only green-background branded thumbnail. These have near-zero CTR. Any video with this thumbnail will never be clicked from Browse or Suggested. Needs to be replaced immediately for any video that still receives impressions.

**2. Titles are generic instructional — not incident-based**
"How to Turn Your Habits Into a System That Actually Works" competes with 50,000 identical videos. This channel's strength is personal, specific incidents — but the titles aren't using them.

- Better: "I Tried to Rely on Discipline for 3 Years. Here's What Actually Changed."

"8. Why Humans Need Closure to Move On?" — the "8." prefix signals you're deep in a series, discouraging new viewers from clicking.

The opening is too slow. Need to drop the viewer into the specific incident or observation immediately.

### What's working

## @breathofpoetry

**Channel:** 8 subscribers · 30 videos

### Key Numbers
- Most recent video "Safe and Alive: A Poetry Essay on Real Love" — **No views** after 5 days
- Best-ever video: "Whispers of the Deserted Mind || Routine" — 23 views (2 years ago)
- Same video was uploaded twice (with and without a number prefix), each getting low views

### Issues

**1. Most recent video has zero views — likely near-zero impressions**
Either YouTube isn't serving it at all (new channel with no momentum) or CTR is so low it's invisible. With 8 subscribers, every new video starts from zero with no pre-warmed audience.

**2. Poem titles don't lead with the emotionally resonant line**
"Safe and Alive: A Poetry Essay on Real Love" describes the poem but doesn't make anyone feel anything.
Better: lead with the line that makes someone stop and say "this is about me."
Example: "Love Doesn't Announce Itself — A Poem" vs. "Safe and Alive: A Poetry Essay on Real Love"

**3. "||" separator style looks like old SEO spam**
"Whispers of the Deserted Mind || Routine || Spoken Word Poetry" — this formatting was common in 2018 YouTube SEO and now reads as low-trust. Drop it.

**4. Branded text-only thumbnails on older videos**
Same problem as Life channel — "Breath of Poetry" text on a colored background provides no reason to click.

**5. Strategic question: is long-form the right focus here?**
30 videos, 8 subscribers, 0 views on the most recent upload. Poetry is the hardest niche on YouTube for long-form — it requires a strong existing audience. The Shorts format (60s spoken word clips, text overlays, atmospheric visuals) is where poetry actually works on YouTube right now. Recommend making Shorts the primary output for this channel and treating long-form as a bonus.

## Cross-Channel Patterns

**The single root cause across all three channels: thumbnails don't stop the scroll.**
CTR ranges from 0.3% to 1.1% across all channels. The target is 5%+. YouTube is showing the content (5,700+ impressions on DS alone) — people just aren't clicking.

**What to do first, in order of impact:**

1. **Fix the DS Tutorial 1/10 thumbnail this week.** It has 5,700 impressions and 0.3% CTR. A new thumbnail with your face and one strong reaction could take it from 17 clicks to 280+ clicks on the same impression pool. Test it.

2. **Kill all "Breath of Poetry & Life" branded thumbnails** on any video with >500 lifetime impressions. Replace with face + hook text.

3. **Remove the series number prefix** from all titles across all channels ("Tutorial 1/10", "8. Why Humans Need Closure"). Let the title stand alone.

4. **Rewrite the opening 60 seconds** of Tutorial 1/10 and the most recent Life video. Both have AVD under 1 minute on 7–16 min videos. Start with the specific problem or incident — no title card monologue.

5. **Double down on Shorts.** Across all three channels, Shorts are outperforming long-form in views. The DS Shorts are the top content in the last 48 hours. The system is working for Shorts — it's not working for long-form yet.
