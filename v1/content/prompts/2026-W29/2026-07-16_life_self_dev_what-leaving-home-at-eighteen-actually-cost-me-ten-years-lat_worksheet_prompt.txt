Create a reflective worksheet PDF (A4, portrait) for "The Inventory Audit: What Your Ambitions Are Really Made Of"

Layout:
- Header (top 15%): Soft gradient background. Title + subtitle about personal growth.
- 4 sections vertically stacked, each with:
  - Section title (warm orange or teal accent)
  - Reflective prompt in italics
  - Blank space for handwritten response (30-50% of section height)
  - Dividing line between sections

Colors: Warm cream background (#FFFBF0), teal accents (#0D9488), dark gray text

Sections:
1. Map Your Generation Lag
   - Name one topic where you sprinted ahead of your parents fast enough that a conversation now feels like 'a bad phone line.' What is it?
   - Write the last argument you had with a parent. Strip out blame — what's the actual lag underneath (exposure gap, not malice)?
   - What did your parents spend their energy building instead of running the race beside you? Name the specific thing.
   - Does naming this as 'structural, not personal' change how angry you feel right now? Write one sentence either way.

2. Price What Your Education Actually Bought
   - Finish this: 'If I had a smaller education, I would have been happy with ___.' Be specific — a job, a street, a Sunday.
   - Name one person whose life you envy precisely because they stayed small — what does their ordinary Tuesday look like?
   - What door did your education open that you can never close again, even though you wish you sometimes could?
   - Would you trade the exposure back for the peace? Answer honestly, no resolution required.

3. Trace Your Ambition Back to Its Source
   - Before you 'caught' your current ambitions, what was your one visible option growing up — the thing you mistook for a calling?
   - Name the first specific person who showed you an ambition you'd never been offered. What was it, and what did it do to you that week?
   - List two things nobody ever put in front of you as a kid (art, travel, a trade, entrepreneurship — anything). How would your inventory look different if they had?
   - Is the goal you're chasing right now actually yours, or is it borrowed from someone you stood near at the right moment?

4. Find the Love Underneath the Ambition
   - Under your current hustle — career, fitness, content, whatever — name the actual want driving it: recognition, love returned, proving something to someone specific.
   - Write about one time you gave something (love, effort, loyalty) with open arms and it didn't come back. What wall did you build afterward?
   - Which of your walls are protection you still need, and which have outlived the threat that built them?
   - What has your own version of 'lyrics for songs that don't exist yet' been — the small, half-embarrassing outlet where your unspent love actually goes?


Footer: "Reply with your answers — your reflections help others find their entry point."

Optional: Soft watercolor accent in corner.
