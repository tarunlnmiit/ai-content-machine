   Go through your current stack orchestrator, tracking, serving, monitoring, and for each one you're paying for or over-provisioning, name the free swap from the post (Prefect for Airflow, self-hosted MLflow for a capped free tier, FastAPI+Uvicorn for a managed endpoint, Evidently for Arize/Fiddler) and estimate the monthly dollar difference.
