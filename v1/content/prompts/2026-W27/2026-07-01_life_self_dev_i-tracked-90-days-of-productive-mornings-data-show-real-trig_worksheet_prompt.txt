Create a reflective worksheet PDF (A4, portrait) for "Find Your First-Task Latency Number"

Layout:
- Header (top 15%): Soft gradient background. Title + subtitle about personal growth.
- 4 sections vertically stacked, each with:
  - Section title (warm orange or teal accent)
  - Reflective prompt in italics
  - Blank space for handwritten response (30-50% of section height)
  - Dividing line between sections

Colors: Warm cream background (#FFFBF0), teal accents (#0D9488), dark gray text

Sections:
1. Track Your Gap
   - For the next 7 mornings, log: wake time, what you touched first (phone/journal/body movement), and minutes until first focused task
   - Rate each day 1-10 for 'productive' before bed, no overthinking
   - Circle the mornings where first-task latency was under 20 minutes — how many landed a high rating?
   - Circle the mornings phone was the first touch — what's the average rating there?

2. Find Your Doom-Scroll Morning
   - Name one specific morning where you did everything 'right' (gym, coffee, desk on time) but the day still failed — what was the very first thing you looked at that day?
   - What content or feed pulled your attention before you'd chosen your own first thought?
   - How many minutes did that first input eat before you stood up?
   - If you'd swapped that input for silence, what's the one task you'd have started instead?

3. Audit Your Sleep Debt vs. Wake Time
   - Write down 2 recent mornings: one where you forced an early wake on poor sleep, one where you woke naturally after paying off sleep debt
   - Compare first-task latency and output between the two — which variable actually explains the gap?
   - What time did you go to bed both nights? Be exact.
   - Are you currently chasing an hour on the clock, or the hours of sleep behind it?

4. Design Your No-Phone-First-Hour Test
   - Pick tomorrow's first task before you go to sleep tonight — write it down now
   - Decide where the phone sleeps tonight (not arm's reach) and commit to the spot
   - Predict your productive-day rating for tomorrow if you hit zero screen time in hour one
   - After the day, log the actual rating and compare it to your phone-first average


Footer: "Reply with your answers — your reflections help others find their entry point."

Optional: Soft watercolor accent in corner.
