## Poem

Birds don't look back.
I feed what stays.
Fish rise for anyone.
The fat one shoves in.
That's not loyalty.
That's hunger.
Main bhi aisa hoon.
I rise for hands.
Mostly yours.
Call it love, then.
Kaun poochta hai fark?
The old man doesn't.
You're allowed
to want this much.
