## Essay Caption (Joey Kidney template)

I planned this trip like a dataset. Best light for photos, ghat timings, the one sunset shot everyone tells you to get in Pushkar. I got it in a few seconds.

I've spent ten years finding patterns in numbers, so I notice when the pattern breaks. On this trip it broke almost immediately — the moments I kept coming back to weren't the ones I'd researched.

They were the koi taking bread from my hand. A musician nobody photographed. A shop we spent a long stretch in and [CONFIRM: verify true] bought nothing from.

None of that was on the itinerary. That's probably why it stayed.

I still make the itinerary — noticing a habit doesn't kill it. What changed is what I do with the slack: I stopped filling every open hour, so there was room for a market shop or a stranger's music to happen. Tell me the part of your last trip you didn't plan for and loved anyway.
