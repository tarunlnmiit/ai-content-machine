Subject: The 6-second video I almost deleted
Preview: And what it taught me about making things that actually land.

Last year I shot a 6-second video at 11pm. I almost erased it before anyone saw. It became the most me thing I've ever put on the internet.

I wrote this week about that — and about the paradox underneath it. The authentic act creates an audience. The audience creates a mirror. The mirror makes you self-conscious. Suddenly the thing that worked because it was unfiltered becomes performance.

I don't have a clean fix. But I've found a small practice: one thing per week with no audience at all. Not a draft, not a future post. Something that exists just for the doing.

The piece is built around a poem. It's the kind of writing I almost never share because it feels too unfinished — which is probably exactly why I had to.
