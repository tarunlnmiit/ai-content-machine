There's a move you keep holding back.

Last year I shot a 6-second video at 11pm. Almost deleted it before anyone could see.

It became the most me thing I've ever put online.

The pieces that hit — the ones people reply to with 'this is exactly me' — are almost always the ones I nearly didn't post.

The thing nobody tells you: self-consciousness doesn't go away before you begin. It disappears inside the doing.

There's a draft in your Notes right now. A thing you've been refining until the moment passes.

The room won't clear.

Dance anyway.
