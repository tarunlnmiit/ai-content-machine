A LETTER TO YOUR YOUNGER SELF

Dear 16-Year-Old You

Trace the blame, the love, the quiet start, and the castle you built — then write the letter your future self owes you.

Trace Your Own 'First Blame'

Name one path — art, business, trade, sport — you never dreamed of at 16, simply because no one put it in front of you.

Who was your only role model growing up? Whose ambitions did you catch like a fever once you left that bubble?

Name the mediocre, peaceful life you sometimes wonder if you'd have been happier living.

Write one sentence to your parents about a choice they made for you without meaning harm.

Roads Not Taken
2
Map Where Love Became Your Engine

List the ambitions in your life driven by love, unrequited love, or heartbreak — not pure ambition.

Describe one time your love was not reciprocated. What did it build in you instead of break?

Where has self-doubt gotten dangerously loud in your head? Name the moment.

What would you make — write, build, start — out of your next heartbreak instead of just surviving it?

Love As Fuel

Find Your 'Writing Articles Online' Moment

Name the small, unglamorous start you're underrating right now — lyrics for songs that don't exist, a journal, a half-built app.

What two cents do you have to offer the world that you haven't shared yet?

What decision — like moving to Germany with a hidden second agenda — could serve a practical need and your real pursuit?

Describe one person among the 1,000 who'd follow your story if you started telling it publicly.

Small Starts
4
Name Your Castle and Its Monsters

Where do you feel loneliness even surrounded by people — and what wall did you build there?

Name one person you let in who brought chaos. What did letting them go actually look like?

Who are the few who've kept the sanctity of your castle intact? Have you told them that?

Write one line of the letter your future self, ten years from now, would send back to you today.
