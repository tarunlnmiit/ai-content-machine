Price What Your Education Actually Bought

Finish it: "If I'd had a smaller education, I'd be happy with ___." Name the job, the street, the Sunday.

Name one person who stayed small. Describe their ordinary Tuesday, in specifics.

What door did your education open that you can never close again, even wishing you could?

Trace Your Ambition Back to Its Source

Before you caught your current ambitions, what was the one visible option you mistook for a calling?

Name the first person who showed you an ambition you'd never been offered. What did it do to you that week?

List two things nobody ever put in front of you as a kid — art, travel, a trade, a business.

Is the goal you're chasing actually yours, or borrowed from someone you stood near at the right moment?

CAUGHT, NOT CHOSEN
4
Find the Love Underneath the Ambition

Under the hustle — career, fitness, content — name the actual want: recognition, love returned, proving something to someone specific.

Write about one time you gave love with open arms and it didn't come back. What wall went up after?

Which of your walls still protect you, and which have outlived the threat that built them?

What's your version of lyrics for songs that don't exist yet — where your unspent love actually goes?
