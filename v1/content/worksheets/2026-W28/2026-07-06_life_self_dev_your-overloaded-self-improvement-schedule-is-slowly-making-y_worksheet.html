Count Your Habits Like He Counted His

List every tracked habit you run right now — app, spreadsheet, streak. Total count: ___

Circle no more than 4 you'd defend as the ones that actually matter.

For everything not circled — when did you last do it without checking a box first? Could you even tell?

Which habit looks most virtuous on paper but you've never examined its real effect?

Find Your Own '7:40 Call' Moment

Recall the last time a routine made you irritated at someone you love for interrupting it.

What did you choose that day — the checkbox or the person? Be specific.

Is there a closed door right now — partner, kid, friend — who's stopped arguing and just says okay?

Write the sentence you'd say if asked to skip tonight's routine. Does it sound like declining a meeting?

3
Find What's Quietly Starving

Name one thing you used to enjoy that produces no loggable number — has it quietly stopped?

Do you feel low-grade guilt in hours that don't feed a tracker cell? Name the last one.

What's your version of his Sunday cricket reruns — time labeled "wasted," one podcast away from getting cut?

If you cut that dead time, what filled it — and was that actually an improvement?

4
Run the Cut-to-4 Experiment

Pick your 4 keepers — his were one workout, one 5-min journal, 30 min writing, sleep by 11.

Write exactly what you're dropping this week — name each one, not "the extras."

Estimate hours reclaimed per week. Where does that time go — a person, a hobby, sleep?

Set a 3-week check-in date. What's the one honest metric you'll check — besides a streak count?
