---
title: "Dance When the Playlist Ends"
niche: poetry_quotes
---

There's a video I shot last year. Six seconds. Me, alone in my apartment at 11pm, doing something embarrassing enough that I almost deleted it. I didn't. We all have a move we keep holding back.

---

> You have to dance
> when the playlist ends
> when the room empties
> when no one is left to grade your form.
>
> You have to move
> even with the wrong body
> the untrained hands
> the borrowed rhythm.
>
> Most of us wait
> for permission we never get —
> for applause that is always
> one brave act away.
>
> Dance anyway.
> Not for the encore.
> For the ache in your chest
> that finally has somewhere to go.

---

The room won't clear. Dance anyway.
