I spent a decade working with things that can be measured — data, models, dashboards. Then a poem found a nerve no dataset ever mapped, and something released. I didn't plan for that. I don't have a framework for it. I've stopped pretending the two lives are separate.

---

> poetry dips its fingers
> in every colour
> the grief colour
> the 2am colour
> the colour of a commute
> where nothing happens
> and you feel, inexplicably, okay
>
> it finds you in the technical —
> in elegant code that reads like a volta
> in the commit message
> with grief hidden inside it
>
> it finds you in the ordinary —
> burnt toast over the sink
> the smell specific enough
> to be a poem
> before the day takes over
>
> it is patient
> it waits in the unremarkable
> until you are open
> just barely
>
> the door doesn't need to be wide
> a crack is enough

---

Poetry was already there. It was never waiting for an invitation.
