## Hook — 5 variants (08 §hook-polish: write 5, pick best, drivers from the 6-driver taxonomy)

1. ✅ **PICK** — *Identity + Curiosity* — "I'm a data scientist who writes novels. Same skill runs both — and it's the exact skill that makes AI actually useful." *(Sets up the reveal without giving it away)*
2. *Contrarian* — "You're not bad at AI. You're bad at asking questions." *(Direct, challenges the viewer's assumption)*
3. *Curiosity* — "Nobody tells you the one skill that actually makes ChatGPT useful." *(Word-trigger: "nobody tells you")*
4. *Storytelling* — "I spent ten years asking questions for a living. Turns out that's the whole AI skill." *(Authority via lived experience)*
5. *Authority/Proof* — "Ten years in data science taught me the exact skill AI rewards. Steal it." *("Steal this" word-trigger)*

**Driver:** Identity bridge + curiosity gap. Honesty note: no tool/model is named as superior; the claim is about question quality, not AI capability. Hard cut, face to camera, no warm-up.

## 4-Beat Script (08 §carousel→reel map)

### BEAT 1 — HOOK (0–4s)
- **VO:** "I'm a data scientist who writes novels. Same skill runs both — and it's the exact skill that makes AI actually useful."
- **On-screen (burn-in):** `10 YEARS OF DATA SCIENCE → 1 SKILL FOR AI`
- **Cue:** Face to camera, hard cut in. Re-record 5×, keep the sharpest.

### BEAT 2 — PROBLEM (4–10s)
- **VO:** "You type 'write me a story,' get something bland, and blame the AI. That's not the model failing — that's a vague prompt. Garbage in, garbage out."
- **On-screen:** `garbage in → garbage out`
- **Cue:** Static face or simple text-on-screen card, no B-roll needed. Name the pain (bland output), then reframe it (it's the prompt, not the tool).

### BEAT 3 — VALUE / PROOF (10–22s)
- **VO:** "Here's the exact prompt that unstuck a novel I'd been stuck on for years — a role, a specific ask, real constraints. 'Take this seed idea. Generate eight escalating twists. Rank them by how much they'd hook a reader.' It hands me doors. I pick."
- **On-screen (burn-in, staged — this IS the proof, shown verbatim so the CTA can reference it):**
  1. `ROLE + ASK + CONSTRAINTS + YOUR JUDGMENT`
  2. `"Generate 8 escalating twists. Rank by hook potential."`
- **Cue:** Face to camera throughout; text card 2 holds long enough to read (viewer needs to register "I just watched the real prompt" for beat 4's CTA to land).

### BEAT 4 — PAYOFF + CTA (22–30s)
- **VO:** "Internalize that pattern, and free ChatGPT or Claude gets shockingly capable. Your answer is capped by your question. Comment AI and I'll send you the exact prompt you just saw — plus two more."
- **On-screen:** `Your question is the ceiling.` → `Comment "AI" → the prompt you just saw + 2 more`
- **Loop:** end frame = the "Your question is the ceiling" text card → invites rewatch.

## Caption (06 formula — the caption IS the product)

Comment "AI" and I'll send you the exact prompt you just saw — plus 2 more 👆

I've used these for ten years across data science and fiction writing — same underlying skill, different output.

The 3 prompts (verbatim — copy, paste, fill in the brackets):

1. Beat the blank page —
"Take this seed idea: [YOUR IDEA]. Generate 8 escalating 'what if' twists that raise the emotional stakes and make the story harder and more interesting. Rank them by how much they'd hook a reader."

2. Debug like a senior engineer's next to you —
"You are a pragmatic senior engineer. I'll share code and errors. Explain the root cause in plain terms, give the smallest correct fix, and point out any edge case or security issue I'm missing. Ask before assuming requirements."

3. Think on paper on the hard days —
"I'm feeling [WHAT YOU FEEL]. Ask me three gentle questions to help me separate the facts from the fears, then suggest one small, kind next step. Be warm and brief."

The pattern behind all three: a role, a specific ask, real constraints, and room left for your own judgment. That's the whole method.
