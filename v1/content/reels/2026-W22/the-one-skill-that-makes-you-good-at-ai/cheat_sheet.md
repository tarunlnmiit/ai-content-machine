# 3 Prompts That Actually Work — Cheat Sheet

*Companion to: "The One Skill That Makes You Good at AI (It's Not What You Think)"*
*DM this when someone comments AI.*

The pattern behind all three: give the AI a role, a specific ask (not "help me" — a concrete deliverable), constraints (tone, length, what to avoid), and leave room for your own judgment. Copy, paste, fill in the brackets.

---

**1. Beat the blank page** *(for any creative project you're stuck on)*

```
Take this seed idea: [YOUR IDEA]. Generate 8 escalating "what if" twists
that raise the emotional stakes and make the story harder and more
interesting. Rank them by how much they'd hook a reader.
```

**2. Debug like you've got a senior engineer next to you**

```
You are a pragmatic senior engineer. I'll share code and errors. Explain
the root cause in plain terms, give the smallest correct fix, and point
out any edge case or security issue I'm missing. Ask before assuming
requirements.
```

**3. Think on paper on the days the words won't come**

```
I'm feeling [WHAT YOU FEEL]. Ask me three gentle questions to help me
separate the facts from the fears, then suggest one small, kind next
step. Be warm and brief.
```

---

That's the whole method. Steal these, tweak the structure, build your own.
