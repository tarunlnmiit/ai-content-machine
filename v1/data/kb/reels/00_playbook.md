# 00 — Playbook (strategy spine)

## Why the winning reel worked

The reference winner ("I automated the worst part of job hunting") stacked four things:

1. A **visceral, universal problem** (job hunting is miserable).
2. A **real, working artifact** shown on screen — a script scanning 130+ career pages, scoring roles 0–100, texting matches. Proof, not claims.
3. A **trust trifecta**: free, open-source, runs locally, no subscription, no data sent anywhere — pre-empts the "is this a scam" reflex.
4. **One frictionless CTA**: comment one keyword → DM the repo.

Takeaway: the formula isn't the magic — the honest, demonstrable result is. Everything downstream inherits this.

## The 5 beats (the unit all formats compress to)

| # | Beat | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook (0–3s) | Stop the scroll. Bold claim or big text, legible muted. |
| 2 | Problem | Name the pain, specific and relatable. |
| 3 | Reveal + proof | What it is + show it actually working. |
| 4 | Payoff | Why it matters / the result. |
| 5 | CTA | ONE action — comment a keyword, get the DM. |

This is also the shape your clip-finder should look for inside long-form video, and the shape the Remotion script→scene step should fill.

## The brand lane: builder who also teaches

- **Build reels** (things you actually made, shown working) earn trust and are your most defensible lane.
- **Teach reels / carousels** (how-to, listicles, roadmaps) earn reach and saves.
- Suggested mix: ~50% build, ~35% teach, ~15% curiosity.

## What the data confirmed (across 24 saved reels)

- Top performers are "connect Claude to a tool/MCP/skill → a free thing you can run," with the repo/skill in the DM. This is exactly the winner's mechanic.
- Carousels and "save this" posts earn outsized shares/saves — the reach play.
- The keyword→DM CTA is universal and visibly converts.
- Income-claim and over-hype reels pull big raw numbers but the wrong crowd (see `04_honesty_guardrail.md`).

## Per-piece checklist

- [ ] Hook is one of the 8 archetypes (`01_hook_library.md`), legible muted in frame 1.
- [ ] Real proof on screen — built it or can run it. No claims you can't show.
- [ ] 5 beats present; for reels ≤45s, hard cut at 0–3s, captions burned in.
- [ ] Trust trifecta where true: free / open / local.
- [ ] ONE keyword CTA; pin the keyword comment.
- [ ] End on the result (loop it), not "bye".
- [ ] Passed the honesty guardrail.
