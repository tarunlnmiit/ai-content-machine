# 01 — Emotional Hook / Angle Library (poetry + life)

Emotional archetypes that earn recognition (saves/shares), drawn from the account's proven voice
(`twitter_hook_patterns.json`, patterns tagged `life`/`poetry`) and the retention/engagement wins. Use these for
blog titles, YouTube titles, reel hooks, and carousel covers — not just reels. Each: pattern → real example →
performance note → niche fit.

> Sibling of the tech `data/kb/reels/01_hook_library.md`. That one is build/tech ("Tool + Tool = outcome"). This one
> is emotional. Do not mix them across niches.

### 1. Contrarian Mirror — uncomfortable truth about how we behave
`[Observation about human behaviour] + [why it's uncomfortable] + [the truth underneath]`
- "There's a moment, usually quiet, usually late, when the performance stops making sense."
- Performance: highest like+share combo in the set (tension = engagement). **Poetry + Life.**

### 2. Open Question / Redefinition — question that seems answered but isn't
`And honestly, what is [concept]? [Why the common definition is wrong.] [Your redefinition.]`
- "What is rest, really? Not the absence of work. The absence of the next thing."
- Performance: strong for abstract themes (success, identity, rest). **Poetry + Life.**

### 3. Ellipsis / Continuation — start mid-thought, imply backstory
`[Statement that implies a story]… [the actual point]`
- "Life teaches you it isn't fair, but…"
- Performance: above-average shares; curiosity pulls the click. **Poetry + Life.**

### 4. Protective Imperative — abstract virtue via a visceral metaphor
`The thing I learned: protect [abstract noun] like you protect [visceral thing].`
- "Protect your hope like you protect your most prized possession."
- Performance: high likes, lower public shares — resonates privately (saves). **Poetry + Life.**

### 5. Social Proof Inversion — shared failure → relatability → action
`I [relatable admission]. [Why that's okay.] [What to do instead.]`
- "I had five systems and wrote nothing. Turns out the system was the avoidance."
- Performance: strong shares — people forward what they feel but won't say. **Life (and Poetry).**

### 6. Bold Declaration — short, loud, no softening
`[BLUNT CLAIM]. [One-line why.] [Your specific angle.]`
- "Most advice fails because it arrived before your pain did."
- Performance: high RT ratio — people share declarations. **Life.**

### 7. Specific Loss / Named Feeling — name what people can't name (poetry-native)
`[A precise, small, devastating detail.]`
- "How they said your name, the last time, like it still meant something."
- Performance: poetry's highest-retention driver — specificity = being seen. **Poetry.**

### 8. Permission / Anti-toxic-positivity — opposite of "good vibes only"
`You don't have to [optimize/fix/heal faster]. [What's true instead.]`
- "Don't fix me. Just stay."
- Performance: counter-programs the feed's forced positivity; strong saves. **Poetry + Life.**

### 9. Fear Trigger — name what they're already scared of but won't say
`[Specific fear] + [why it's valid] + [the harder truth underneath]`
- "The thing nobody tells you about anxiety: it gets quieter, but it doesn't leave."
- "You're not afraid of failure. You're afraid that failure means you were never good enough."
- Performance: high saves + comments; people tag others silently. Fear that names itself = relief. **Poetry + Life.**

### 10. Curiosity Gap / Revelation — "I didn't know until..."
`[Common belief] + [what's actually true] + [why it changes everything]`
- "I thought silence meant peace. It didn't. It meant I'd run out of things to pretend."
- "What I thought was strength turned out to be the inability to ask for help."
- Performance: strong shares; curiosity drives the click, resonance drives the save. **Poetry + Life.**

## How to use across formats
- **Blog/YouTube title:** pick the archetype matching the piece's emotional core (Specific Loss for grief poems;
  Social Proof Inversion / Bold Declaration for life).
- **Reel/clip hook:** first 3s = the archetype as big legible text (spoken-word poetry auto-plays muted).
- **Carousel cover:** archetypes 1, 2, 5, 6 are carousel-native for life.
- Test 2-3 hook variants per piece; keep the winner; log it in `03_swipe_file.md`.
