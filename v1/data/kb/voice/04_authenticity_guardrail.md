# 04 — Authenticity Guardrail (poetry + life)

Non-negotiable QA gate, the voice-niche companion to the tech `reels/04_honesty_guardrail.md`. Applies at the
**blog stage** (so it propagates to every derivative) and again at final review of each format. Poetry/life travel on
*trust and recognition*; the fastest way to lose both is performed emotion.

## The rules
1. **No toxic positivity.** Don't resolve real pain with a silver lining. Sadness can stand on its own; the
   permission close is "you're not alone," not "but everything happens for a reason."
2. **No manufactured urgency.** No "you NEED to hear this," no fake stakes. Earn attention with specificity, not pressure.
3. **Real cost, real stories.** Every life "I struggled with X" / poetry lived moment must be genuine. Invented
   vulnerability reads as fake and kills the relationship faster than no story at all.
4. **Specific over abstract.** A concrete detail (a name, a chair, a distracted dinner) beats a general truth. If a
   line could be on any motivational account, cut it.
5. **Permission, not prescription.** Close by naming the feeling / giving permission to stop trying harder — not with
   a 5-step fix. (Life can offer a framework, but lead with the mechanism and end with grace.)
6. **No clichés / banned words.** Avoid "in conclusion," "dive into," "leverage," "game-changer," "synergy," and
   greeting-card phrasing ("live, laugh, love"; "everything happens for a reason").

## Pass/fail checklist (run before publishing any format)
- [ ] No silver-lining bow on real pain.
- [ ] No manufactured urgency / fake stakes.
- [ ] Every personal story is true.
- [ ] At least one concrete, specific detail carries the piece.
- [ ] Ends on permission/naming, not a hard prescription or hard sell.
- [ ] Reads as *this person*, not a motivational template. If a stranger could've posted it, rewrite it.
