# 00 — Voice Playbook (poetry + life strategy spine)

The reels KB sells *built things*. This KB sells *being understood*. Poetry and life don't go viral by proving a
tool works — they go viral when a stranger feels **seen** and forwards the piece to say "this is me / this is you."

## Why this creator's poetry/life already works (the evidence)

- **Poetry retains.** `data/analytics/youtube_analytics_Breath_of_Poetry.md`: "Loneliness" 24.9% retention,
  "Hurts Like Inferno" 31.1%, avg ~32.7% — very high for spoken word. Winners name a *specific* emotion
  (isolation, yearning, self-realization), not an abstract one.
- **Life saves.** IG carousels hit 308 / 302 likes (eng score ~50.9, the highest of any format) when they read as
  confession → mechanism → permission. People screenshot one slide and send it to a friend.
- The unique angle that's irreplaceable: **a data scientist who also feels** — precision + vulnerability. Lean on it.

## The emotional arc (the unit all formats compress to)

### Poetry
| # | Beat | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Specific observation | A lived, concrete moment — not an abstract truth. "There's a moment, usually late, when the performance stops making sense." |
| 2 | The poem as evidence | Concrete nouns, sparse metaphor, internal contradiction. The poem *is* the proof, not decoration. |
| 3 | Permission close | Name the feeling so the reader knows they're not alone. Not a solution — a "I see you in this." |

### Life
| # | Beat | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confession | A pattern the reader recognizes as theirs. "I had five productivity apps and wrote nothing." |
| 2 | Mechanism | *Why* it happens — neuroscience / psychology / attention economics. This is the screenshot-able line. |
| 3 | Framework + caveat | 1 practice (or a short set), each tied to a real cost story. Systems fail; practices return. |
| 4 | Permission to stop trying harder | The anti-motivational close. "Start with the one that stings." |

These arcs are also what the clip-finder should hunt for in long-form, and what the Remotion script→scene step fills.

## What makes each niche travel

- **Poetry → saves + shares of recognition.** Specificity is the multiplier. "shitting all over the hearts of the
  good ones" beats "people hurt authentic people." Shares happen because someone wants to say *I'm not alone*.
- **Life → saves + screenshots.** The mechanism line (the *why*) is the share trigger; the framework is the save
  trigger. Relatability + a name for the pain.

## Proven formats (reference only — we still produce every format per niche)

Poetry has performed best as **reels** (rewatch/retention); life as **carousels** (save-bait, screenshot). Keep this
as context when writing, but per current direction the machine still generates all formats for both niches.

## Per-piece checklist

- [ ] Hook is one of the emotional archetypes (`01_hook_library.md`).
- [ ] Specific over abstract — a concrete moment/noun, not a general truth.
- [ ] Poetry: ends on permission/naming, not a fix. Life: ends on permission to stop trying harder.
- [ ] One clear, gentle CTA (save/share/reply/subscribe — not a hard sell).
- [ ] Passed the authenticity guardrail (`04`): no toxic positivity, no manufactured urgency, real cost shown.
