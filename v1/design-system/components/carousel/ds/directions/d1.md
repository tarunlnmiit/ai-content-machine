# D1 — Backchannel — LOCKED TEMPLATE PACK

Native-UI mimicry: the slide is not a graphic *about* a Slack DM — it *is* one. Two panelists,
dark mode, the exact weight of a real thread: header, bubbles, pinned quote, reactions, an inert
composer. Nothing announces itself as designed; that restraint is the craft. The "Marcus is
typing…" indicator at the foot of every non-final slide is the direction's signature cliffhanger —
the reader waits for a reply that never comes, so they swipe.

## Visual language

- **Palette**: ground `#1A1D21`, panel/topbar `#202329`, bubble `#2B2E33`, "me"-bubble `#2C3B47`,
  ink `#E8E6E3` / muted `#8A8D91` / dim `#5C5F63`. Avatar gradient
  `linear-gradient(155deg,#3D5F75 0%,#6B8FA8 55%,#96BAD0 100%)` — the brand blues, the only color
  accent in an otherwise monochrome dark UI. Flagged/starred exhibit cards use a desaturated
  rose/green pair (`#C4636B`/`#6FA88A`) exactly as Slack's own reaction-colored UI would — this is
  the one direction permitted to break the "no red/green" ledger rule, because here red/green are
  interface semantics (flag/approve), not decoration.
- **Fonts**: -apple-system/Segoe UI/Helvetica stack for all chrome and bubbles (system-UI
  authenticity is the point — a real Slack client never loads a webfont for message text);
  'Space Grotesk', sans-serif for the one moment the UI breaks register: the pinned headline.
- **Texture**: none. No paper grain, no doodles. A native app has no texture layer — the realism
  IS the texture.

## Chrome mapping (exporter contract, re-skinned into Slack's world)

The counter badge normally anchors top-left; here it lives inside the topbar itself, exactly where
a real client's channel-info affordance would sit, so the back-chevron is dropped (a static
carousel has nowhere to navigate back to) and the avatar/name group shifts right to clear it.
`.follow-tag`, swipe arrow, `.save-tag`, and `.cliffhanger` are absolutely-positioned overlay pills
at the exact mandated coordinates, styled to read as Slack's own UI chrome (translucent dark pills,
soft shadow) rather than a bolted-on graphic. `.progress-row`/`.progress-seg`/`.fill` sit in-flow
directly under the topbar, restyled as Slack's own thin unread-indicator strip.

## Rules

- Never drop the pinned-quote block, reaction row, or composer to simplify a slide — a Slack DM
  without its chrome is just a caption.
- Never use the same body template on two adjacent slides.
- The typing cliffhanger's name must be one of the two panelists already introduced on the hook
  slide — don't invent a third person.
- Hook headline: 5-8 words, hard cap, lives only in `.pin-headline` on the hook slide.
- Body copy per slide: under 30 words total across all bubbles/cards.
- Red/green in `.exhibit.bad`/`.exhibit.good` is the one sanctioned use of that pairing in this
  system — reaction-colored UI semantics, not decoration; do not extend red/green anywhere else.
