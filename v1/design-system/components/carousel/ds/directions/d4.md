# D4 — Redacted Memo — LOCKED TEMPLATE PACK

Risograph duotone: the leaked internal file, not a graphic about one. A halftone dot grain (the
real visual signature of duotone offset printing, not a texture layer bolted on), an orange
classification bar, TO/FROM/FILE fields in a true typewriter face, a redaction bar that withholds
one word completely, and a circular ink-blue verification seal placed like a real rubber stamp.
Flat and bureaucratic on purpose — the opposite direction from the retired scrapbook look. Two
colors, one restraint: a genuine duotone print process physically cannot produce more than two
plate colors plus paper, and honoring that constraint is what keeps this from sliding into generic
"distressed poster" cliché.

## Visual language

- **Palette**: paper `#EFEDE7`, ink `#1E1B2E`, secondary ink `#4A4658`, marginalia `#8A8578`, the
  one warm plate color `#E2572A` (classification bar, halftone dots, accents only), the brand's
  ink-blue `#3D5F75` (verification seal + one exhibit head only). No third accent, no gradient —
  three colors plus paper, full stop.
- **Fonts**: 'Courier Prime', monospace — a true typewriter face, everywhere, no exceptions. This
  is a memo, not a display document; nothing in this direction is allowed to look designed.
- **Texture**: the halftone dot grain (`radial-gradient` dot pattern, opacity ~.10, orange) is
  mandatory on every slide — it is the print evidence, not decoration, and its absence is the
  single fastest way to make this direction look like a flat mockup instead of a printed page.

mockup's own design — kept exactly as the counter, sitting inside `.fields` rather than floating
as an app chip, because a real memo would never have a floating progress chip. `.follow-tag`,
swipe arrow, `.save-tag`, and `.cliffhanger` are drawn as additional memo-field rows and footer
marginalia so they read as part of the document, not overlay UI bolted on top.

## Rules

- The halftone dot layer is mandatory on every single slide, no exceptions — its absence is the
  fastest way this direction reads as a flat mockup instead of a printed artifact.
- The redaction bar withholds exactly one word, never a phrase and never a partial word.
- Only three colors exist in this direction: paper `#EFEDE7`/ink `#1E1B2E`, orange `#E2572A`, and
  blue `#3D5F75` — no fourth color, no gradient, ever.
- Hook headline: 5-8 words, hard cap, exactly one word redacted.
- Body copy per slide: under 30 words total.
- Never use the same body template on two adjacent slides.
