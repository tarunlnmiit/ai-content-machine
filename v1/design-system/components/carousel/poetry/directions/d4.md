# Poetry Carousel — Direction D4: "Room to Breathe" — LOCKED TEMPLATE PACK

Base philosophy: *ma* — the Japanese principle of purposeful negative space — treats emptiness as
an active compositional element, not unused canvas. Most of every frame is deliberately vacant;
words are pushed to the margin, asymmetric, small, earned rather than assumed. The power move
isn't size, it's placement. A small circular seal mark closes each composition like a printer's
chop. Nowhere to hide an imprecise margin — the margin IS the design.

## Visual language

- **Palette**: cream `#F8F4EA`, bronze `#8A6E30` (seal ring, hairline, progress fill), muted
  taupe-gold `#B7AD8E` (chrome labels, dimmed progress dots), ink `#241F1A` (primary verse),
  dimmed ink `#A9A08B` (receding/secondary verse).
- **Fonts**: Playfair Display at modest, readable sizes for verse — never the dramatic scale of
  other directions; the power move is placement, not size. `DM Sans` for chrome labels.
- **Ground**: flat cream, no texture, no grain — the emptiness itself is the ground. Composition
  is asymmetric (a narrow right-hand column or lower-right anchor), never centered.

## How these templates work (read before generating)

1. Every archetype ships as a complete markup block + CSS. Fill only the `<!-- SLOT: ... -->`
   comments. Do not center a composition that's meant to be asymmetric, do not fill empty space
   with a doodle or texture "to help" — the vacancy is the point, not a gap to be closed.
2. The **seal mark** (circular bronze ring with a center dot) recurs on every slide, alternating
   corner (bottom-left on some archetypes, bottom-right on others per the template) — it is this
   direction's signature device, distinct from the required counter-badge.
3. The **shared overlay chrome** below is defined once — include it verbatim on every slide.
   Progress renders as small DOTS (not bars) to match this direction's restraint — the required
   `.progress-seg`/`.fill` class names still apply, just styled circular.
4. Vertical rhythm inside a slide (a line, a long pause, another line, a longer pause) should read
   as measured, not accidental — treat unequal gaps between fragments as a deliberate spatial
   translation of the poem's own line breaks, never as leftover space.

A five-word line pushed into the lower-right third, a single bronze hairline marking the column
edge, a circular seal bottom-left, the remaining two-thirds of the frame left empty on purpose.

## Rules

- **Hook headline** = the lower-right anchor line, &le;8 words, broken across 2-3 short lines.
  Body slides carry stanza fragments — the generic &lt;30-word cap applies only to gloss/chrome
  text, never to verse fragments, which stay short by the direction's own discipline anyway.
- Verse sizes stay modest (15-27px range across this pack) — never scale a line up for drama; the
  power move is placement and asymmetry, not size. If a slide feels like it needs a bigger word,
  that is a signal to route it to D2 instead, not to break this direction's scale discipline.
- Alternate which side (left/right) the text column and seal anchor to, slide to slide, so the
  asymmetry itself doesn't calcify into a new kind of symmetry across the deck.
- Never fill the empty two-thirds with a doodle, texture, or second color field — vacancy is the
  design, not unfinished space.
