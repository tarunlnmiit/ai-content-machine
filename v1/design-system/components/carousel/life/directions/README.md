# Life Carousel — Direction Routing

Four locked template packs live in this folder: [`d1.md`](d1.md) (The Group Chat), [`d2.md`](d2.md)
(Load-Bearing Type), [`d3.md`](d3.md) (After Hours), [`d4.md`](d4.md) (Minutes of the Meeting). All
four are approved and stay in rotation — pick ONE per carousel based on what the content actually
is, not on visual variety for its own sake. Never split a single carousel across two directions.

## Routing rubric

| Direction | Route here when the content is... | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **D1 — The Group Chat** | Relational, dialogue-driven, second-person confession — a story that happened *between* the narrator and someone else (a friend, a partner, a coworker who said the thing). Content that would naturally get texted to someone. | The iMessage disguise only works when there's a real exchange to borrow the format's authority from. Content with no other party in it has nothing to put in the incoming bubbles. |
| **D2 — Load-Bearing Type** | A single hard-hitting reframe, mantra, or one-sentence realization — essay content that resolves to ONE quotable line the reader should screenshot. Low narrative complexity, high aphorism density. | This direction has no room for a story arc or a second voice — it succeeds only when the content genuinely compresses to a sentence worth setting at 60px. Forcing a multi-beat narrative into it starves the deck of things to say. |
| **D3 — After Hours** | Introspective, night-thoughts, grief-adjacent, or anything sitting in stillness rather than event — content about what someone didn't say, didn't do, or is quietly carrying. Low plot, high interiority. | The dark, centered, near-silent register reads as contemplative honesty. Routing high-energy or list-heavy content here wastes the direction's entire point, which is restraint. |
| **D4 — Minutes of the Meeting** | Work, meeting, and office-dynamics content — anything about a workplace interaction, a professional habit, or a decision made (or avoided) in a room with other people at a table. | The bureaucratic-form disguise depends on the content having a plausible reason to exist inside meeting minutes. Content with no office/meeting frame has no document to hide inside. |

## Default pick

**When a piece of content could plausibly route two ways, default to D1 (The Group Chat).** It has
the widest content coverage of the four — almost any confession can be reframed as a text exchange
even when it wasn't originally relational, because a "friend asks what happened" incoming bubble
can front-load context for nearly any beat. D2, D3, and D4 are narrower, purpose-built registers;
reach for them when the content's own shape (a single aphorism, a night-thoughts essay, an office
story) obviously matches, not by default.
