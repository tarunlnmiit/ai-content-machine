How It Works
Runs at midnight.
Done by morning.
Every night, while you sleep, the script does three things:
1
Scrapes 130+ career pages
Finds every new role posted since the last run
2
Scores each role 0–100
LLM reads your resume + JD and scores fit — skills, seniority, tech stack
3
Texts you your top matches
Only roles above your threshold land in Telegram. No noise.
No manual work. No missed opportunities. No job board doomscrolling.
