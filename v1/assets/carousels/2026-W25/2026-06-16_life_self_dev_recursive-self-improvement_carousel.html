The 5 Loops
  • 1
    Fix Your Information Diet First
    Curate what curates you. Switch from consuming content to consuming mental models — they compound; motivation evaporates.
  • 2
    Build Habit Stacks, Not Habits
    One keystone behavior (usually physical) unlocks 3–4 downstream habits. Skip the anchor, the chain collapses.
  • 3
    Build a Review System That Reviews Itself
    Monthly meta-reviews teach you how you think — not just what you did. Add: "What question am I not asking?"

  • 4
    Find Skills That Unlock Other Skills
    Writing clearly forces thinking clearly → accelerates learning in every domain. That's a root node, not a leaf. Root nodes get priority.
  • 5
    Rewrite the Identity Narrative First
    Behavior follows identity. If your self-concept doesn't update, you'll unconsciously self-sabotage back to whoever you used to be.
Depth ranking
Loops 1–4 are tactical. Loop 5 is architectural. If you're plateauing, start there — it changes your defaults instead of fighting them.

Start the loop today
Three questions — in order. Be honest with #1.
  • 1
    At what level am I optimizing? Habit, system, or identity? Name it. Most people are on the habit layer and wonder why it doesn't compound.
  • 2
    What's my actual keystone behavior? The one that — when skipped for three days — makes two other habits disappear as well.
  • 3
    Does my self-story match the evidence? Write: "I am someone who ___" then list three behaviors that prove it today — not someday.
