Poetry
The Hangover
That Won't Lift
a poem about love that takes away all your senses
and refuses to give them back
"
The Feeling
"
One day you're fine.
The next, someone's laugh
is living rent-free
in your head.
The trouble isn't the feeling.
It's what comes after — when it refuses to lift.
The Poem
B
love has taken away
all my senses
like that of alcohol
but your intoxication
doesn't want to go away
my heart keeps enjoying
the hangover you created
your twinkling eyes
your enlightening smile
your ebony hair
are stuck with me
like a newborn sticks to her mother
∞
Four Metaphors
When Love Becomes
Part of Your Physics
🦋
like a moth attracted to the flame
pure pull — no deliberation, no weighing outcomes
🌊
like waves to the shore
returning, always returning — momentum not intention
❄️
like cold to the winter
inseparable by nature — not by decision
🌈
like a rainbow after the rain
inevitable — and always a little too late
The Collision
their ecosystems
have collided
No ground to stand on.
No sky to cover my head with.
The tools you've always used to navigate
your own interior life no longer apply.
Not broken. Not weak.
Just — somewhere entirely new.
The Takeaway
Some people become
part of your physics.
1
The pull exists before it's reasoned
moths don't choose flames — they're wired toward light
2
You return without deciding to
like waves — pure momentum, not intention
3
The fault line isn't a failure
it's evidence something actually matters enough to overturn the system
4
The heart wants what the heart wants
not an excuse — a report from inside the collision
