The 4-part pattern
  1. 1 A role — "you are a senior engineer"
  2. 2 A specific ask — not "help me," but "give me 8 ranked options"
  3. 3 Constraints — tone, length, what to avoid
  4. 4 Room for your judgment — AI generates, you decide
