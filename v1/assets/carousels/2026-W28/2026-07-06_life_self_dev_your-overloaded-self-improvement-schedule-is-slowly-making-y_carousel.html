The one that surprised me

The habit that hurt me most wasn't a lazy one.

It was tracking everything. A novel doesn't produce a loggable number the way 20 pages of a self-help book does — so fiction quietly starved to death in my system. I stopped reading it. Not decided to. Stopped.

What changed
I became someone who audits his own life like a quarterly report. My wife noticed before I did.
