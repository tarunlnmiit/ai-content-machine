Monday Morning, 7:14 AM

It Finds You
in the Ordinary

Poetry is not elevated experience.

It is ordinary experience,
seen clearly for a second.

The grey of a commute. The brown of bad coffee. One Tuesday afternoon that feels, somehow, okay.

The Pattern

It Finds You
When You Stop
Looking

Poetry comes sideways.

In a lyric heard forty times that suddenly means something different.

The door doesn't need to be wide.

A crack is enough.
