7 Simple Habits That Quietly Rewired My Productivity

No systems. No templates. Just practices that actually stick.

B
Breath of Life
→

I spent years chasing the perfect system.

Notion templates. Time blocks. Weekly reviews with five-step sub-reviews.

I was organized. I was busy. I wasn't moving.

→

Then I stripped back to 7 simple habits.

No apps. No subscriptions. No morning routine at 4 AM.

Each one creates a moment of contact with how you spend your attention.

→
Habits #1 & #2

The Shutdown Ritual

Four minutes: scan tasks, note what's unfinished, write what you did. Tell your nervous system: we can stop tracking this now.

The Brain Dump

Every Sunday: 20 minutes. Empty your head onto paper. Not organized, just out.

→
Habits #3 & #4

Eat the Frog

The hardest task first. Just one. The rest of the day feels lighter not because you did more—because you stopped carrying the weight.

The Done List

Track what happened, not what's left. We consistently underestimate what we do. This corrects that.

→
Habits #5, #6, #7

90-Minute Deep Block

Long enough to warm up. Long enough to go deep. One per day.

Tomorrow's Three

Write your three priorities tonight. Future you won't have to decide.

Track One Metric

One number per week. What gets measured, gets noticed.

→

Start with the one that stings.

That's the right one.

These work because they create contact with how you spend your attention. Honest, practiced every day.
