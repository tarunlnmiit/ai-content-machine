80% on Cleaning

Visualization? Friday afternoon stuff.

This is backwards.

  • Bad data breaks everything — yes.
  • Bad visualization buries the truth.
  • Clear plots change minds in seconds.
→

Matplotlib + Seaborn

Core tools. Not decoration.

  • Matplotlib: The canvas. You control everything.
  • Seaborn: The paintbrush. Beautiful defaults + stats.

Learn to frame your data. Make it impossible to miss.

→

The Narrative Choice

Same data. Three different stories.

  • Line chart: Implies trend or sequence
  • Bar chart: Emphasizes rankings
  • Scatter: Reveals relationships

Choose the plot that matches your finding.

→

Mistakes That Tank Trust

  • Too many colors: Humans can't distinguish them
  • Dual y-axes: Let you lie about correlation
  • 3D plots: Impossible to read accurately
  • No context: Add labels. State your claim.
→

The Winning Formula

  • 1. Create: fig, ax = plt.subplots()
  • 2. Plot: ax.plot() or sns.scatter()
  • 3. Label: axes, title, legend
  • 4. Save: dpi=300, bbox_inches='tight'

Precision in every step. That's how you earn credibility.
