Notice.
Thoughts driven
by exteriority.
No care about what lies within.
wearing layers
of masks themselves
ready to tear down
the true faces
shitting all over
the hearts of
the good ones
Only to make
themselves happy.
Only to make
themselves happy.
The mask doesn't just hide —
it needs to.
The waking up isn't despair.
It's the end of being fooled.
Tarun Gupta · @breathofpoetry
