The Hangover That Won't Lift
When the heart takes over the keys

A poem about love that refuses
to schedule its own departure

It starts quietly

One day you're fine.
The next, someone's laugh is
living rent-free in your head.
The part of your brain that runs the show
has quietly handed over the keys.

Love has taken away
all my senses
like that of alcohol
effects of booze reside
for a short period
but your intoxication
doesn't want to go away
my heart keeps enjoying the hangover you created

What makes it stay

Vague longing is forgettable.
But twinkling eyes. Ebony hair.
An enlightening smile.
That's a face. A real one.

like a moth attracted to the flame
like waves to the shore
like cold to the winter
like a rainbow after the rain

The collision

Earlier, mind and heart worked in sync.

Then: their ecosystems
have collided.

No ground to stand on.
No sky to cover my head with.

Not a malfunction

When the system is overturned,
that's a signal.
The only honest one you'll get
that something actually matters.
That's not a problem to solve.
That's the experience itself.
