# Brag Plan: "The 5-Part Prompt Anatomy" (AI Prompting for Travel)

**Chosen hook:** "Paris on my list for 6 years. One prompt. Three secret sites."
**Hook formula:** POV + Hyper-specificity (lived six-year stake + concrete, verifiable payoff)
**Tone preset:** app-store
**One-line angle:** Six years of a stalled Paris bucket list vs. one engineered prompt — showcase the 5-part prompt anatomy as clean feature cards that turn ChatGPT from a listicle machine into a personal Parisian historian.

---

## What is this app?
Not an app — a Data Science/Tech essay that reverse-engineers the exact ChatGPT prompt the author used to plan Paris. It breaks "good prompting" into a 5-part anatomy: (1) assign a role, (2) define the task with surgical precision, (3) explicitly exclude the obvious, (4) dictate output format, (5) demand verification. The brag: a working prompt engineer's actual method, packaged as five features you can copy.

## The angle
Play it exactly like a product feature reel for a real app — because the "product" here is a repeatable method. The video treats the 5-part prompt anatomy as five clean feature cards, each stated as a benefit, building toward the payoff the hook promised: three hidden Paris sites no listicle surfaces. Specific to this essay, not generic — it uses the essay's real mechanics ("No Bordeaux. Surprise me.", "interrogated it", the contractor mental model) and its real transformation (generic Eiffel-Tower answers → an obsessive local historian).

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Full-screen, app-store clean: **"Paris on my list for 6 years."** lands first and holds, then **"One prompt. Three secret sites."** snaps under it. Title-case, medium weight, generous space. This is the POV + hyper-specificity opener — a lived six-year stake plus a concrete, countable payoff the video will actually deliver.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The problem card, stated as a product would state the pain it kills: *"Ask ChatGPT for 'best things in Paris' → you get the same 10 listicle answers since 2009."*
- The 5-part anatomy revealed as five feature cards, one by one, each name + one-line benefit:
  **Assign a Role** · **Define the Task** · **Exclude the Obvious** · **Dictate the Format** · **Demand Verification.**
- The single sharpest line from the essay, held as a feature caption: *"No Bordeaux. Surprise me."* — the exclusion step made concrete.
- The payoff card: ChatGPT no longer returns the Eiffel Tower — it returns three lesser-known sites in a clean Markdown table, delivering exactly what the hook promised.

## Outro / punchline
App-store CTA close on the mental model: **"Your prompt is your contract. The more precise it is, the better the build."** Then the essay's title as the "product name," clean.

## User flow worth showing
none — no app or flow exists. Centerpiece is the 5-part anatomy shown as sequential feature cards plus the before/after of the AI's output (generic listicle → three hidden sites in a table).

- Music treatment: enter under the hook at moderate volume, hold steady through the feature cards, small lift into the payoff card, clean fade under the outro CTA. No drop, no chaos.

For the five sequential feature cards, use a beat-grid window — snap card arrivals to every other beat (roughly 0.7-0.9s apart) so each name+benefit holds to its read floor; do NOT snap them to consecutive fast beats.
