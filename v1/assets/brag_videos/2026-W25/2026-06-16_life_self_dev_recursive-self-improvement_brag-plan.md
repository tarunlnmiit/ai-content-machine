# Brag Plan: "The Recursive Loop" (essay — Life & Self-Development)

**Chosen hook:** "Most people fix their habits. The smart ones fix what fixes their habits."
**Hook formula:** curiosity gap + contrarian (fused — names a hidden second level AND overturns the default self-improvement frame)
**Tone preset:** freeform: systems-diagram (base fallback: `polished`)
**Angle (one line):** Self-improvement has a second-order game — the loops that improve your improving; render each of the 5 as a self-feeding node graph and argue most people optimize the wrong level, landing on identity as the deepest loop.

---

## What is this app?
Not an app — a personal essay, "5 Loops That Make Self-Improvement Self-Sustaining." Its argument: single-layer self-improvement plateaus because you keep spending willpower per habit. The unlock is *recursive* improvement — improving the system that does the improving, so the loops start feeding themselves. Five loops, ranked tactical → identity-deep. The brag is the idea itself: a genuinely different way to think about getting better, drawn as the systems it describes.

## The angle
The essay is literally about loops that feed themselves — so the video's form IS its argument. Instead of a talking-head or a list of tips, each loop is a live node-and-edge diagram whose output arrow curls back into its own input. The premise the video sells: everyone optimizes the *level of the habit*; the recursive move is optimizing the *level above it*. We show four loops fast, then reveal the fifth (identity) as the loop that contains all the others. Specific to this piece: uses the essay's own "second-order game," "optimizing the wrong level," and "leaf node vs. root" language, and its skill-graph and identity-loop are drawn, not narrated.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black-warm canvas. Two lines land in sequence, contrarian pivot on the second:
**"Most people fix their habits."** (holds ~1.2s) → **"The smart ones fix what fixes their habits."** — as the word "fixes what fixes" is said, a small node draws an arrow back into itself (the first self-loop). The recursion is stated AND drawn in the same two seconds. Longest-held scene after the outro.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The wrong-level reveal.** A tall stack of nodes labeled *habit · habit · habit*; a single arrow lights up one level higher, labeled *the system that builds habits*. On-screen: *"You're optimizing the wrong level."* The essay's central claim, made spatial.
- **Loops feeding themselves, one by one.** Three compact self-loop diagrams snap in in sequence — **Inputs** (consume *models*, not *content* → next choice improves), **Habit stacks** (one keystone node fans out to 3 downstream nodes), **Reviews** (a review box with an arrow curling back to sharpen its own prompts). Each is a node graph whose output edge returns to its input. Restrained labels, one idea each.
- **The skill graph.** The essay's own image: a network where one node ("writing / clear thinking") has far more edges than the rest; that hub lights up and its edges cascade outward. On-screen: *"Some skills unlock other skills. Find the roots, not the leaves."*

## Outro / punchline
Everything recedes to one large node labeled **IDENTITY**, and the four earlier loops redraw *inside* it — the loop that contains the loops. Final line, resolution color:
**"The deepest loop is the story you tell about who you are. Change that, and the rest runs in your favor."**
Then the essay's title card: *5 Loops That Make Self-Improvement Self-Sustaining.*

## User flow worth showing
none — no app or flow exists. The centerpiece is the conceptual structure drawn as self-feeding node graphs (the essay's own argument made visual).

## Tone
- Preset: freeform: systems-diagram (closest built-in base: `polished`)
- Creative direction: animated self-feeding loops on a node graph — restrained palette, technical-but-warm, an idea diagrammed rather than decorated
- Interpretation: pacing is measured, not chaotic — each loop gets enough hold to read as a *system*, not a flash. Motion is purposeful (edges draw, arrows curl back, hubs cascade); it clarifies the argument instead of hyping it. Confidence through restraint: few colors, clean type, one concept per scene. The recursion is shown by geometry (output → input), never by clutter.

## Share copy (draft)
Most self-improvement is a treadmill because you're optimizing the wrong level. The unlock is recursive: five loops that make getting better *self-sustaining* — drawn as the systems they actually are.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse, warm, thinking-tempo bed — low and steady, more "focus" than "hype." Supports the diagrams; never drives them.
- Music: a slow, minimal electronic/acoustic bed with a soft pulse (mood: contemplative-systems, not corporate-upbeat). If a bundled track fits, prefer one with a gentle steady beat so edge-draws can align; final track/tempo to be confirmed at composition time.
- Music treatment: enter low under the hook, hold flat and quiet through the four loops, a single soft lift as the four loops redraw inside IDENTITY, then settle under the closing line so the words land last. No drop, no percussive spike.
- Music cue guidance: target one soft strong-cue at the identity redraw (~scene 5). Beat-grid windows for the three sequential self-loops in Scene 3 — snap each loop's *entrance* to a beat but hold each fully on screen to its read floor (do not chase the beat with the labels). Exact timestamps + track to be detected at composition time.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let bass/RMS make the active amber edge glow breathe slightly as it draws; do not add waveform bars or reactive clutter.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched. A soft draw/whoosh as each edge completes; a quiet low "settle" as the arrow curls back into its own input (the recursion beat); one faint chime as the hub node lights in the skill graph. Restraint over density.
- Audio-coupled moments: each self-loop's return-arrow (the curl-back) should have a small sound so the recursion *registers*; the hub cascade in the skill graph; the four loops snapping into the IDENTITY node.
- Restraint rule: audio must never turn this into a hype reel. No riser-into-drop, no aggressive stabs. The subject is thinking about thinking — sound stays calm and deliberate.
