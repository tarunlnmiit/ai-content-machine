Most people fix their habits. The smart ones fix what fixes their habits.

Single-layer self-improvement plateaus because you keep spending willpower one habit at a time. The unlock is recursive — you improve the system that does the improving, and the loops start feeding themselves.

Five of them, drawn as the systems they actually are:
• Inputs → consume models, not content
• Habits → one keystone unlocks the chain
• Reviews → a review that sharpens itself
• Skills → find the roots, not the leaves
• Identity → the loop that contains all the others

Stop optimizing the level of the habit. Optimize the level above it.
