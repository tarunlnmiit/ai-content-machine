# Brag Plan: "Dance When the Playlist Ends" (Poetry/Quotes)

**Chosen hook:** "POV: 11pm, alone, doing the move you always hide."
**Hook formula:** POV + cold open (drops the viewer into the poem's literal opening confession — the almost-deleted 11pm clip — rather than the "dance like nobody's watching" cliché the poem quietly rewrites)
**Tone preset:** freeform: **kinetic-confessional** (motion-led, intimate, direct-address — closest built-in base is `chaotic` toned down toward `default` for legibility)
**Angle (one line):** The six-second clip I almost deleted becomes the argument — stop waiting for permission that never comes and move "for the ache in your chest that finally has somewhere to go."

## What is this?
Not an app — a short poem (published as "Dance When the Playlist Ends"). Four stanzas that reframe the worn "dance like nobody's watching" line into something sharper and more private: you have to move *when the room empties*, with the wrong body and borrowed rhythm, *before* any permission or applause arrives. The frame around it is a real confession: a six-second clip the writer shot alone at 11pm and almost deleted.

## The angle
The premise is a confession delivered as motion, not a product demo. The video is the poem *acting itself out* — restless, handheld, one thought slamming into the next — and it lands on the poem's own thesis: don't dance for the encore, dance "for the ache in your chest that finally has somewhere to go." Specific to this piece, not generic: it uses the poem's actual lines and the actual almost-deleted-clip framing. Social proof is the published headline, not a stat.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
**"POV: 11pm, alone, doing the move you always hide."** Text slams in on a dark, close, off-kilter frame with a single warm rim of light — the feeling of a lamp-lit apartment after midnight. The line holds long enough to read fully (~2s) while the frame breathes with a subtle handheld drift. It IS the cold open: the viewer is already inside the scene before the poem starts.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The reframe that sets the whole poem apart, delivered as the first real line: *"You have to dance when the playlist ends — when no one is left to grade your form."* The kinetic energy starts here.
- The permission line — the argumentative peak — as a hard, held beat: *"Most of us wait for permission we never get. Applause that's always one brave act away."*
- The turn: *"Dance anyway. Not for the encore."* — motion peaks, then a deliberate hold before the last line lands.

## Outro / punchline
The poem's closing image, held clean and still after all the motion settles: **"For the ache in your chest that finally has somewhere to go."** Then the poem's title / the "The room won't clear. Dance anyway." tag as the resolution.

## User flow worth showing
none — no app or flow exists. Centerpiece is the poem's lines rendered as kinetic type, framed by the almost-deleted-clip confession and the published title as proof.

## Tone
- Preset: chaotic (toned down toward default — kinetic energy, but readable, not a hype reel)
- Creative direction: kinetic-confessional — handheld, restless, intimate; direct address; motion that mirrors a body that can't stay still
- Interpretation: type is alive and always slightly moving; entrances are fast and physical (slam, drift, jitter); but every line holds to its read floor. The energy is a person *dancing*, not a metrics montage — so the motion is human and a little imperfect, never mechanical. One still, quiet beat at the end to let the ache land.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, close, rhythmic bed — a slow pulse that gives the kinetic type a beat to move against, not a hype drop. Intimate, like something playing quietly in a dark room.
- Music: slow, warm acoustic/lo-fi with a soft but present pulse (a track with a gentle beat grid so line reveals can sit on the beat). Enters under the hook, builds subtly through the middle, and — critically — drops to near-silence under the final line so the ache lands in quiet.
- Music treatment: low under the POV hook, a small lift as the poem's motion peaks at "Dance anyway," then fade to near-silence for the closing image.
- Music cue guidance: track to be selected at composition time; beats/cues detected then (`npx hyperframes beats` or `analyze_music_cues.py`). Target 1 strong cue for the "Dance anyway" peak (~14s). Beat-grid the sequential poem-line reveals to every-other-beat so they stay readable, not snapped too fast.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let the bass pulse make the type's drift/sway breathe, so the motion feels coupled to the music. No waveform bars.
- SFX posture: sparse. One soft, intimate texture at most (a low room-tone swell under the hook). No hard clicks or whooshes — the subject is private, not punchy.
- Audio-coupled moments: the "Dance anyway" peak lands on a beat; the closing line lands *off* the beat, in the drop to silence.
- Restraint rule: no drop, no hype-reel bass slam, no percussive stinger on the closing line. The last line is quiet on purpose.
