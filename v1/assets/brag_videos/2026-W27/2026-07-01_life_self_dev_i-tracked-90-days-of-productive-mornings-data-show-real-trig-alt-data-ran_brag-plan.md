# Brag Plan: "The data ran the launch" — Variant A (app-store) for the 90-days-of-mornings essay

## What is this?
Not an app — a personal essay published in the Medium publication **Know Thyself Heal Thyself**:
"I Woke at 6AM Every Day for 90 Days. The Data Said Wake Time Was Never the Point." The essay
tracks 90 mornings of self-logged data (wake time, first-task latency, energy) and finds that the
clock never predicted a good day — what you let into your head in the first hour did. The brag is
real writing, really published, and the essay's REAL numbers are the launch material.
**No recorded live URL** (known gap) — the proof card shows the real headline + the "Know Thyself
Heal Thyself" wordmark only, no domain / URL bar anywhere.

## The angle
Present the essay's honest self-tracked numbers as a clean, confident feature-card launch deck —
Playfair Display 900 big numerals in glass-panel cards, brisk app-store cadence. The data speaks
for itself: same wake time, opposite outcomes, driven by first screen touch. Never a chart, never
a bulleted feature list (both banned by the Life niche KB and by the essay's own honesty — these
are real numbers, not product features).

## Tone
- Preset: app-store — clean, confident, feature-card cadence; brisk but not rushed.
- Soft crossfades (~0.45s smooth wipe), NOT hard cuts. Card-forward, brighter, more composed than
  the photographic siblings.
- Playfair Display carries the editorial weight: 700 for headlines/hook, 900 for the 71%/34%
  numerals, 400 italic for the hook turn line. Inter 500/600 for labels/eyebrows.

## Color contract
- Background warm `#1a1208`, card bg (glass) `rgba(35,26,14,0.85)`, border `rgba(245,158,11,0.18)`,
  text `#fdf8f0`, muted `#a0856a`.
- Amber `#f59e0b` = the "good" data color — the under-20-min stat card, the 71% numeral, the hook
  turn, the proof wordmark.
- Warm-red `#ef4444` = the "bad" data color — LOAD-BEARING here (first build in the series to use
  it heavily): the scroll-first stat card and the 34% numeral.
- Sage `#86efac` = reserved EXCLUSIVELY for the final outro line (S5b) — "who picked your first
  thought." Nowhere else.

## Storyboard (soft crossfades, ~21.25s total, vertical 1080x1920, alternating tracks 1/2)
- **S1 hook (0 – 3.5):** "I woke at 6am for 90 days." (Playfair 700) → amber italic turn: "Wake
  time was never the point."
- **S2 (3.05 – 6.85):** photo `notebook-laptop.png` behind a semi-transparent glass card (slow
  push on the photo): "90 mornings — Wake time, first-task latency, energy. Logged by hand."
- **S3 (6.4 – 10.9):** two paired stat cards, stacked — amber "First task under 20 min → good day
  ~90%", warm-red "Scroll first → bad day ~80%" — beneath: "Same wake time. Opposite outcomes."
- **S4 (10.45 – 14.95):** two big Playfair 900 numerals — "71%" amber (no-phone first hour), "34%"
  warm-red (phone first) — beneath: "'Productive' self-rating. Regardless of wake time."
- **S5 proof (14.5 – 17.7):** restrained glass card — "Published in" + "Know Thyself Heal Thyself"
  wordmark (amber, NOT sage) + the real unchanged headline. NO URL bar / domain anywhere.
- **S5b outro (17.25 – 21.25):** sage line — "The real trigger wasn't the clock. It was who picked
  your first thought."

## Duration: ~21.25s
Six scenes, soft crossfades. Reading floors respected — S3 (two cards + subline) and S4 (numerals
+ subline) each get 4.5s so both cards clear the floor before the wipe.

## Real numbers (verbatim, not re-rounded)
- Under-20-min first-task latency → good day ~90%.
- Phone-scroll-first → bad day ~80%.
- No-phone-first-hour mornings → 71% "productive" self-rating.
- Phone-first mornings → 34%.
