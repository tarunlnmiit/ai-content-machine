# Brag Plan: "OUTPUT" — Variant B (yc-parody) for the self-help essay

## What is this?
Not an app — a personal essay published in the Medium publication **ILLUMINATION**:
"The 3 Hours You Spend on Self-Help Content Are Making You Worse at Self-Improvement."
The essay argues that consuming self-improvement content is sophisticated procrastination —
that the real move is five minutes of honest output before the phone loads. The brag is real
writing, really published. **No recorded live URL** — the proof card shows the real headline +
the ILLUMINATION wordmark only, no domain text anywhere.

## The angle
The video is a mock anti-startup launch deck. The essay is itself about the productization of
self-improvement, so parodying the polished-startup-launch format IS the argument — the medium
becomes the message. "OUTPUT" is presented as a product that keeps negating itself: no app, no
newsletter, no dashboard. Every product slide is deadpan-straight; the content self-destructs.

## Tone
- Preset: yc-parody — mock-corporate confidence that keeps undercutting itself.
- Hard cuts (NOT crossfades). Minimal motion — quick entrance tweens inside each scene, but the
  cut between scenes is a hard slam. Flatter, more graphic than the photographic siblings.
- Typographic flip vs the deadpan sibling: **Inter 900 uppercase is the DOMINANT typeface** — it
  carries the wordmark and every spec-card headline (product-deck voice). Playfair Display is
  demoted to exactly ONE line — the S4 manifesto — so that single editorial moment cuts through
  the mock-corporate deck.

## Color contract
- Background warm `#1a1208`, card bg `#231a0e`, text `#fdf8f0`, muted `#a0856a`.
- Amber `#f59e0b` is the "fake brand color" for the OUTPUT wordmark.
- Warm-red `#ef4444` is LOAD-BEARING here: the metric-failure / strikethrough accent — used on
  "Progress shipped: 0" (S2) and the underlined word "procrastination" (S4). Nowhere else.
- Sage `#86efac` is reserved EXCLUSIVELY for the final proof beat — the ILLUMINATION wordmark in S5.

## Storyboard (hard cuts, ~19s total, vertical 1080x1920, all scenes on track 1, back-to-back)
- **S1 hook (0 – 3.0):** hard cut in. Near-black background, static. Centered wordmark, Inter 900
  uppercase amber: "OUTPUT." Tagline below, Inter 400: "The morning routine that replaces your
  morning-routine content."
- **S2 (3.0 – 7.0):** spec card on `#231a0e`. "Average user: 3 hrs/day of self-improvement
  content." → stamped beneath in warm-red: "Progress shipped: 0."
- **S3 (7.0 – 11.0):** photo (notebook + laptop) framed like a clinical feature-spec card:
  "3 lines. 2 minutes. Before the phone."
- **S4 (11.0 – 15.5):** the one Playfair italic moment — a manifesto/testimonial: "Most self-help
  content is sophisticated procrastination dressed up as discipline." with "procrastination"
  underlined in warm-red.
- **S5 outro (15.5 – 19.0):** "OUTPUT. No app. No newsletter. No dashboard." → proof tag: "As seen
  in" + "ILLUMINATION" wordmark (sage) + the real unchanged headline. NO URL bar / domain anywhere.

## Duration: ~19s
Five scenes, hard cuts. Reading floors respected — S4 (the 11-word manifesto) gets the longest
hold at 4.5s so it clears its floor before the cut.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm acoustic bed (NOT a corporate track — the visuals/copy carry the parody, not the
  music). Kept low; nothing swells.
- Source: `v1/assets/audio/bgm/life/itYCLFXGQMc_[No Copyright Music] I Just Want Quiet - Slow Acou.mp3`,
  offset ~30s into the source (different from the sibling builds).
- Treatment: trim to 19.0s, `volume=0.20, afade in 1.0s, afade out st=17.5 d=1.5` (slightly faster
  fades than the deadpan siblings, since this video uses hard cuts). Root-level `<audio class="clip">`.
