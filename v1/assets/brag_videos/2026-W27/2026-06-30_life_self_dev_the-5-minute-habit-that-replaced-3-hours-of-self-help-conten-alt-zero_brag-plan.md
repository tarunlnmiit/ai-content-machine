# Brag Plan: "Zero" (app-store) for the self-help-content essay

## What is this?
Not an app — a personal essay published in the Medium publication **ILLUMINATION**:
"The 3 Hours You Spend on Self-Help Content Are Making You Worse at Self-Improvement." The essay
argues that consuming self-help content is sophisticated procrastination, and that a five-minute
daily practice of honest *output* beats hours of borrowed frameworks. The brag is real writing,
really published. Headline is UNCHANGED. **No recorded live URL** (known gap) — the proof card
shows ONLY the real headline + the "ILLUMINATION" wordmark, no domain / URL bar anywhere.

## The angle
Open on the single word "Zero" — that is the hook and the whole thesis in one number: the
correlation between the self-help content watched and what actually changed. Then a restrained
data-forward card deck, an honest manifesto pull-quote, the proof, and a sage-toned closer.
Never a chart, never a bulleted feature list.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store — clean, confident, card-forward; brisk but composed.
- Soft crossfades (~0.45s smooth wipe), NOT hard cuts. Adjacent scenes alternate tracks 1/2.
- Playfair Display carries the editorial weight: 900 for the "Zero" numeral, 700 for statement
  lines / headline / outro, 400 italic for the pull-quote. Inter 500/600 for eyebrows/labels.

## Color contract
- Background warm `#1a1208`, glass card `rgba(35,26,14,0.85)`, border `rgba(245,158,11,0.18)`,
  text `#fdf8f0`, muted `#a0856a`.
- Amber `#f59e0b` = the accent color — the "Zero" numeral, the amber emphasis spans, the proof
  wordmark, the quote marks.
- Sage `#86efac` = reserved EXCLUSIVELY for the final outro clause (S6) — "three hours of input
  never is." Nowhere else.
- No warm-red / no good-bad dichotomy in this build.

## Storyboard (soft crossfades, ~24.3s total, vertical 1080x1920)
- **S1 hook (0 – 2.95):** near-black field, no photo. The single word "Zero" (Playfair 900, amber)
  lands large and clean. This IS the hook.
- **S2 (2.5 – 7.15):** same dark field. "That's the correlation between the content I watched and
  what I changed." (Playfair 700, amber emphasis on the last clause.)
- **S3 (6.7 – 10.65):** notebook+laptop photo behind a semi-transparent glass card (slow push on
  the photo): "30 days — I tracked myself for 30 days like a dataset."
- **S4 (10.2 – 14.45):** manifesto pull-quote (Playfair 400 italic): "Most self-help content is
  sophisticated procrastination dressed up as discipline."
- **S5 proof (14.0 – 18.65):** restrained glass card — "Published in" + "ILLUMINATION" wordmark
  (amber) + the real unchanged headline. NO URL bar / domain anywhere.
- **S6 outro (18.2 – 24.3):** sage line — "Five minutes of honest output is uncomfortable in a way
  three hours of input never is." (last clause sage).

## Duration: ~24.3s
Six scenes, soft crossfades. Reading floors respected — every line's word count checked against
its settled window at ~0.3s/word before locking timing. S2 (12 words → 3.7s window), S6 (16 words
→ 5.2s window) got the longest scenes.

## Audio direction
- Role: clean, confident, warm acoustic bed. Kept low; nothing swells.
- Source: `v1/assets/audio/bgm/life/itYCLFXGQMc_[No Copyright Music] I Just Want Quiet - Slow Acou.mp3`,
