# Brag Plan: "The Quiet Confession" — Variant B (deadpan) for the 90-days-of-mornings essay

## What is this?
Not an app — a personal essay published in the Medium publication **Know Thyself Heal Thyself**:
"I Woke at 6AM Every Day for 90 Days. The Data Said Wake Time Was Never the Point." A data
scientist tracks 90 mornings and finds the clock never mattered — the variable was the gap
between opening his eyes and touching real work. The brag is real writing, really published.
**No recorded live URL exists** — the proof shows only the real, unchanged headline + the
"Know Thyself Heal Thyself" wordmark. No URL bar, no invented domain.

## The angle
The video IS the confession. It reads the essay's own interior voice — a quiet, self-
incriminating admission delivered flat, with long holds and slow crossfades. The two real
photos carry the frame. Only ONE number appears in the whole film (71% vs 34%), so it lands
as the single gut-punch. This is the narrative/photographic counterpart to the stat-dense
app-store siblings.

## Tone
- Preset: deadpan — calm, photographic, first-person; long holds (4–7s/scene), slow
  crossfades (~0.5s dip on a brown stage-ground so seams never flash).
- Interpretation: photos held under a warm color-grade, dim; barely-perceptible drift, no
  Ken-Burns push; large still type; the words land last.
- Colour discipline: amber `#f59e0b` appears ONCE, on the number **"71% vs 34%"**. Sage
  `#86efac` is reserved EXCLUSIVELY for the final outro line. **No warm-red anywhere** — the
  restraint is the point; nothing is colour-coded "bad."

## Hook (first ~5 seconds)
Over the phone-in-bed dawn photo, a Playfair-italic pull-quote, stated flat:
*"I woke up at 6am and doom-scrolled for 20 minutes before I sat up. The day failed."*
Held long enough to sit.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The confession over the same held photo, two staggered Inter lines: **"I'd already checked
  Slack by minute four."** → **"My brain never got to pick its own first thought — someone
  else's inbox did."**
- The one number, near-black field, kinetic slam in amber: **"71% vs 34%"** → beneath it,
  quiet and muted: *phone-first mornings, regardless of what time I woke.*
- The line over the notebook+laptop photo, Playfair: **"You can't gym your way out of a mind
  that already checked out before it stood up."**

## Outro / punchline
A sage line: **"The data said the same thing 90 times."** Then the proof card rises: the
**Know Thyself Heal Thyself** wordmark + the real, unchanged headline. NO URL bar / NO domain
anywhere. Fade to canvas brown.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
Vertical 9:16 for social feeds.

## Duration: ~24.5s
Five scenes. Deadpan wants long cold holds, and the two longest verbatim sentences (an
18-word hook, a 22-word confession split over two staggered lines) need their reading floors
before they can exit. Duration follows the reading floor — the same reason the sibling
"Invoice" and "Quiet Admission" builds settled near 24.5s.

## Storyboard (crossfades 0.5s, tracks alternate 1/2/1/2/1)
- **S1 hook (0 – 5.5, t1):** phone-in-bed photo. Playfair-italic pull-quote.
- **S2 (4.9 – 12.0, t2):** same photo, held (second on-disk copy for lint). Two staggered
  Inter confession lines.
- **S3 (11.4 – 15.7, t1):** near-black field, no photo. "71% vs 34%" (amber, kinetic slam) →
  muted subline.
- **S4 (15.1 – 19.9, t2):** notebook+laptop photo. Playfair statement line.
- **S5 outro (19.3 – 24.5, t1):** dark field. Sage line, then the proof card — Know Thyself
  Heal Thyself wordmark + real headline, NO URL. Fade out.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, calm, sparse LOW bed. Near-silence is acceptable; nothing swells.
- Source: `v1/assets/audio/bgm/life/itYCLFXGQMc_[No Copyright Music] I Just Want Quiet - Slow Acou.mp3`,
  trimmed from a ~65s source offset (distinct from sibling builds).
- Treatment: 24.5s, `volume=0.20, afade in 1.5s, afade out st=22.5 d=2.0`. Flat under the
  whole piece; no beat-grid, no swell. The words land last.

## Share copy (draft)
For 90 days I woke at 6am and tracked every morning. The clock was never the variable — the
first hour was. Phone-first mornings rated 34% productive; phone-free ones, 71%. Know Thyself
Heal Thyself published the whole confession.
