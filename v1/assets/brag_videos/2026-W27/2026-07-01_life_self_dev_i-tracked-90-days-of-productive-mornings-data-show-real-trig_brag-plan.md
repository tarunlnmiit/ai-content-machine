# Brag Plan: "The Twenty Minutes" (cinematic) — for the 90-days-of-mornings essay

## What is this?
Not an app — a personal essay published on the Medium publication **Know Thyself Heal
Thyself**: **"I Woke at 6AM Every Day for 90 Days. The Data Said Wake Time Was Never the
Point."** The brag is: real writing, real data, real pickup — a self-tracked 90-day
experiment that quietly dismantles the entire 5am-productivity gospel.

## The angle
A morning-defeat told cinematically. The video opens on the exact 20 minutes that sank a
whole day — doom-scrolling job postings before the feet hit the floor — names the cost, then
walks the essay's spine: you can't out-gym a checked-out mind, the borrowed urgency of an
inbox opened at minute four, and lands on the reconciling line — the real trigger was never
the clock, it was who picked your first thought. This is the *cinematic* variant: the drama
lives in the warm light and the slow push-ins, not the pace.

## Hook (first 5 seconds)
Photo 1 (phone-in-bed, dawn glow), full-bleed and warm, slow push-in (scale 1.0→1.06). Two
lines land fast: **"I doom-scrolled job postings for 20 minutes at 6am."** then **"The whole
day failed."** — "failed" in amber. The hook must land clean and quick.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The body-truth, over photo 2 (notebook + laptop overhead): *"You can't gym your way out of
  a mind that already checked out before it stood up."* Amber lands on **checked out**.
- The turn — a Playfair italic pull-quote, type-only on the warm canvas:
  *"I'd already checked Slack by minute four. My brain never got to pick its own first
  thought."* Amber on **first thought**.
- The proof: a restrained card — the real headline + the **Know Thyself Heal Thyself**
  wordmark. NO url bar, no fabricated domain — the writing landed somewhere real, shown
  plainly and honestly.

## Outro / punchline
Warm canvas, dimmed. The reconciling line lands in Playfair Display with exactly one sage
accent in the whole film, on **first thought** →
**"The real trigger wasn't the clock. It was who picked your first thought."**

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: quiet morning defeat, elevated stakes, warm push-ins — a data story
  told as a confession
- Interpretation: five scenes, warm full-bleed light on two real photos + a type-only turn,
  slow push-ins, calm holds. The urgency is in the stakes (a day already lost by minute four),
  not in fast cutting.

## Format & duration
Vertical 9:16 — 1080x1920. Target ~24s (reading-time floor drives it above the 19s sketch;
S2 and S3 are 17-word verbatim sentences that each need ~5s of settled reading).

## Visual identity (Life niche look bible)
- Background `#1a1208` (warm deep brown), card bg `#231a0e`
- Text `#fdf8f0` (warm off-white), muted labels `#a0856a`
- Accent amber `#f59e0b` (emphasis words)
- Resolution accent sage `#86efac` — used exactly ONCE, on "first thought" in the outro
- Display: Playfair Display (700 headline, 400 italic pull-quote). Body: Inter (400/500/600)
- Warm amber cast + film grain + vignette throughout (Life niche atmospheric constants)

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, intimate acoustic bed (I Just Want Quiet — slow acoustic)
- Arc: enters low under the hook, holds quiet through the middle, a gentle lift at the proof
  reveal, then fades under the closing line so the words land last
- Treatment: source offset ~215s (fresh window, distinct from all prior builds), trimmed +
  faded with ffmpeg to the exact video duration: volume 0.24, 1.5s fade-in, 2.0s fade-out
