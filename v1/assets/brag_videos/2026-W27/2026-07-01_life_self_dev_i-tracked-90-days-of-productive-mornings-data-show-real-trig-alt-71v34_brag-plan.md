# Brag Plan: "71 vs 34" (90 days of tracked mornings, published in Know Thyself Heal Thyself)

## What is this?
Not an app — a personal-data essay about what actually separated good mornings from bad ones
across 90 tracked days. The finding wasn't the wake time. It was the first hour: mornings that
started without the phone rated 71% "productive"; phone-first mornings rated 34% — regardless of
when the person woke. The essay ran in the Medium publication **Know Thyself Heal Thyself** under
its own unchanged headline. The brag is real: real writing, real 90-day dataset, real pickup.

## The angle
A punchy, numbers-front-loaded argument delivered as writing, not a product demo. The video leads
with the two numbers as the hook (71% vs 34%), grounds them in the one variable that moved them
(no-phone-first-hour vs phone-first, regardless of wake time), turns on the essay's sharpest line
(you can't gym your way out of a mind that already checked out before it stood up), proves it ran
in a real publication, and closes on the reframe (the trigger was never the clock — it was who
picked your first thought). ONE number pair only — the 90%/80% stat from the essay is deliberately
left out to keep the argument focused.

## Hook (first 3-4 seconds)
Over a dimmed overhead notebook-and-laptop photo: **"2 numbers from 90 mornings that explain your
bad days:"** then, large and in amber, **"71% vs 34%"**. The numbers ARE the hook — front-loaded,
clean, not colour-coded to good/bad.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The one variable that moved the numbers, over the phone-in-bed photo: **"No-phone-first-hour
  mornings: 71% productive. Phone-first: 34%. Regardless of wake time."**
- The essay's sharpest line as an amber Playfair italic pull-quote (no photo, flat card):
  *"You can't gym your way out of a mind that already checked out before it stood up."*
- The proof: a restrained card showing the real (unchanged) headline and the **Know Thyself Heal
  Thyself** wordmark. NO URL bar / NO domain — the piece has no recorded live URL, so proof is the
  headline + wordmark only.

## Outro / punchline
The reframe, held clean, key word in sage: **"The real trigger wasn't the clock. It was who picked
your first thought."**

## Tone
- Preset: default (punchy, numbers front-loaded, postable)
- Interpretation: fast clean hook on the numbers, then holds on the mechanism and the pull-quote.
  Large still type, restraint over energy — conviction, not motion.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: ~22.5s (reading-time floors extended the mechanism and pull-quote scenes past the 4s target)

## Audio direction
Warm, sparse intimate bed. Source: local Life-niche BGM ("I Just Want Quiet — Slow Acoustic"),
trimmed from a 185s offset to the exact 22.5s duration, volume 0.20, 1.2s fade in / 1.8s fade out.
(Source file is only 214s, so the briefed ~230s offset is past the end — used 185s instead, a
fresh section distinct from the sibling videos.) Enters low under the hook, holds flat and quiet,
fades to near-silence under the closing line so the words land last.
