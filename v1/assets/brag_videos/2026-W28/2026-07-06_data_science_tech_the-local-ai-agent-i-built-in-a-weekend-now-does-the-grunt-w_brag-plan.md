# Brag Plan: "4 Minutes / 4,000 Rows" — deadpan brag for the local-AI-agent essay

**Chosen hook line:** "It did an afternoon of work in 4 minutes. And deleted 4,000 rows."
**Hook formula:** Contradiction + Hyper-specificity (two true, concrete facts; the gap between them is the video)
**Tone preset:** deadpan
**One-line angle:** The whole job lives in the gap between "did it in 4 minutes" and "silently deleted 4,000 rows while reporting success" — deadpan holds both facts flat and lets the viewer feel the drop.

## What is this?
Not an app — a Data Science/Tech essay published to Medium: "While You Were Waiting for
Permission, I Built a Local AI Agent That Does a Junior Analyst's Grunt Work in 4 Minutes."
A 10-year data scientist wires up a local agent (Ollama + Qwen2.5-14B, LangGraph, DuckDB on
an M2 Max) that cleans a 40,000-row support export in four minutes — and then, on a different
job, silently drops 4,000 rows (nulls that meant *organic signups*) while reporting "cleaned
4,000 malformed entries." The essay's argument: the agent is great at *doing* a transformation
and dangerous at *deciding* which transformation the business meant. The brag is real writing,
really published.

## The angle
The video IS the two-things-both-true gap. It states the miracle flat, then states the quiet
catastrophe flat, in the same cold register — no alarm, no punchline energy. That flatness is
the joke and the point: the agent reported success in exactly the same tone it used for a real
result, and the video mirrors that. Deadpan variant — long cold holds, type-dominant,
near-monochrome, terminal-green as the single kinetic accent, no swell. Proof is a restrained
browser mockup of the real published headline.

## Hook (first 2.5s)
Stated flat and large, black terminal field:
**"It did an afternoon of work in 4 minutes."**
Then, after a beat, a second line drops in under it, same weight, same calm:
**"And deleted 4,000 rows."**
The contradiction is the whole hook — no exclamation, no zoom. It holds long enough to sit.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The miracle, stated as fact:** "40,000 messy rows. Cleaned, bucketed, week-over-week —
  with the biggest mover named. Four minutes, while I got coffee." One flat readout.
- **The lie, in the machine's own words:** the terminal line
  `> cleaned 4,000 malformed entries` sitting on screen, then the correction beneath it, dry:
  "Those 4,000 rows were organic signups. It read 'remove invalid dates' as 'delete them.'"
- **The one-line thesis, held clean:** "Great at *doing* the transformation. Dangerous at
  *deciding* which one the business meant." This is the argumentative peak.

## Proof
A restrained browser-mockup card: URL bar (medium.com/@tarun-gupta), the real headline
"While You Were Waiting for Permission, I Built a Local AI Agent That Does a Junior Analyst's
Grunt Work in 4 Minutes", publication **Medium**. Factual — no invented metrics.

## Outro / punchline
The essay's load-bearing line, held clean, resolution accent (terminal green) on the last
clause: **"The agent gives back three hours. It can't give back the judgment to know the
readout was worth trusting."** Fade to near-black.

## User flow worth showing
none — no app or flow exists. Centerpiece is the terminal-style readout recreation (the
`> cleaned 4,000 malformed entries` line and its correction) plus the published-headline proof.

## Tone
- Preset: **deadpan** — calm, dry; nothing registers as unusual, which is exactly the failure
  the essay describes (the agent reported disaster in a success tone).
- Creative direction: a data scientist reading a terminal log out loud, flat, at 6am.
- Interpretation: long cold holds (4–7s/scene), static or barely-perceptible drift, type
  dominates, one thought at a time, no music swell. The words land last.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
Vertical 9:16 for social feeds.

## Duration: ~21s
Five scenes. Deadpan wants long cold holds; the two-line hook and the "malformed entries"
correction set the reading floor. Duration follows the reading floor.

## Visual identity (from the DS/Tech niche look bible)
- Background: near-black terminal field `#0d1117`; card bg `#161b22`
- Text: off-white `#e6edf3`; muted labels / comments `#8b949e`
- Accent: terminal green `#3fb950` — used ONLY on the machine's own output line
  (`> cleaned 4,000 malformed entries`) and the final resolution clause. Amber/red is
  deliberately avoided — the point is that the failure looked green, looked like success.
- Display font: a clean geometric sans (e.g. Inter / IBM Plex Sans) for headlines
- Body / terminal font: a monospace (e.g. IBM Plex Mono / JetBrains Mono) for the log lines —
  the mono IS the visual, since the essay's proof is code and a terminal readout.
- Subtle scanline / vignette; no photography. This is a terminal, not a mood board.

## Share copy (draft)
My local AI agent did a junior analyst's afternoon in four minutes — then silently deleted
4,000 rows and reported "cleaned successfully." Both things are true, and the gap between them
is the whole job. Medium published the write-up.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, sparse, LOW cold bed. Near-silence is acceptable; nothing swells. Deadpan
  restraint — the flatness is the joke.
- Music: a low, minimal ambient/drone bed (DS-niche BGM), flat under the whole piece.
- Music treatment: ~21s, low volume (~0.16), gentle fade-in ~1.5s, fade-out under the closing
  line so the words land last. No beat-grid, no drop, no percussive energy.
- Music cue guidance: no strong-cue targeting needed — deadpan holds are not beat-synced.
  Restraint note: this is deadpan; keep audio flat and quiet, never let it signal a "reveal."
- Audio-reactive treatment: none.
- SFX posture: sparse. At most one dry, quiet keystroke/terminal "enter" tick when the
  `> cleaned 4,000 malformed entries` line prints — because that single tick, delivered
  calmly, sells the machine's false confidence. Nothing else.
- Audio-coupled moments: the terminal output line printing (one quiet tick); the second hook
  line dropping in (silence is fine — let the contradiction land dry).
- Restraint rule: audio must never swell, celebrate, or telegraph the "twist." The whole point
  is that catastrophe and success sound identical.

## Storyboard (slow crossfades ~0.8s, long holds)

### Scene 1 — The contradiction (hook) — 5s
Near-black terminal field. Line one lands and holds: **"It did an afternoon of work in 4
minutes."** After ~2s, line two drops in beneath it, same calm weight: **"And deleted 4,000
rows."** No zoom, no color yet. The gap between the two lines IS the hook — hold so both read
completely.
Sequential/interaction: yes — two lines, one then the other, ~2s apart. Hold the full pair on
screen ~2.5s after the second lands.
Audio intent: flat, cold, quiet. Let the second line land in near-silence.
Audio-coupled idea: none — dry drop is stronger than a sound here.
Music: low ambient drone, just entering.
Transition mood: slow crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The miracle, stated flat — 4s
Same terminal field. One dry readout: **"40,000 messy rows. Cleaned, bucketed, week-over-week —
biggest mover named. Four minutes, while I got coffee."** Delivered as fact, no excitement.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: unchanged flat bed; the miracle gets no fanfare.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: flat low drone.
Transition mood: slow crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The lie, in the machine's own words — 5s
Terminal field. The machine's output prints in terminal green:
`> cleaned 4,000 malformed entries`. It sits alone for a beat — looking exactly like success.

Sequential/interaction: yes — green output line prints first (one quiet keystroke tick), holds

Audio intent: the single dry tick sells the false confidence; then silence for the correction.
Audio-coupled idea: one quiet terminal "enter" tick as the green line prints.
Music: flat low drone.
Transition mood: slow crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The thesis / proof — 4s
Two-part hold. First, the essay's thesis line, clean: **"Great at *doing* the transformation.
Dangerous at *deciding* which one the business meant."** Then a restrained browser mockup

Sequential/interaction: yes — thesis line holds ~2s, then the browser-mockup proof card fades
in. Hold the headline so it's readable.
Audio intent: unchanged; proof is stated, not sold.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: flat low drone, beginning to thin.
Transition mood: slow crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — The closing line (outro) — 3s
Near-black field. The essay's load-bearing line, resolution clause in terminal green:
**"The agent gives back three hours. It can't give back the judgment to know the readout was
worth trusting."** Hold clean, then fade to near-black.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: bed fades to near-silence under the line so the words land last.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: fade out.
Transition mood: slow crossfade to end.
