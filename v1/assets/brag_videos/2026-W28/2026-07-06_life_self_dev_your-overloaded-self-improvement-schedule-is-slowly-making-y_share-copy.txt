I turned my life into a data pipeline and hit a 91% consistency score. 14 streaks, 11 habits, 3 hours of self-improvement before 9am — a flawless Q1.

Then I read the incident log: declined a movie with my wife to protect a deep-work block, let my mother's call ring because it was Anki time. The weeks I scored highest were the weeks I wrote nothing worth reading and called home least.

The score wasn't the win. It was the damage. Don't ask "am I consistent?" Ask "who is this consistency producing?"
