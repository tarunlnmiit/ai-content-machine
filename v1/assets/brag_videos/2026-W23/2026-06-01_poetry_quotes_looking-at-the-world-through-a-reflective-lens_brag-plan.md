## What is this?
Not an app — a poem published on **Breath of Poetry** (Spotify show), titled "Only to Make Themselves Happy" (slug: looking-at-the-world-through-a-reflective-lens, 2026-06-01). ## The angle
The video IS the diagnosis. It reads the poem's own flat verdict at a funeral pace — no swell, no anger, no resolution. Deadpan here is not comedy; it's the affect of someone who has stopped being surprised. One cold line at a time, big empty space, the poem's actual lines as the visual. The doubled refrain is the whole point: the second "only to make themselves happy" is not a fix — it's a name, held on empty space. Specific to THIS poem: it uses the poem's real lines (masks, false gods, deserving humans, the refrain) and the blog's own framing sentence as the outro, not generic "poetry is deep" filler.

## Audio direction
- Role: cold, sparse, near-silent bed. Deadpan wants absence; near-silence is acceptable and preferred.
- Music: a low, flat drone or slow minimal acoustic — cold register, no warmth, no melody arc. Candidate: a quiet ambient/drone bed if available in the poetry BGM set; otherwise near-silence with room tone. No warm acoustic (that reads Life, not this poem).
- Music treatment: enter low under the hook, hold dead flat through the middle, fade to true silence under the doubled refrain so the repeat lands in silence. No swell, no drop, no beat-grid.
- Music cue guidance: no beat sync — deadpan forbids it. If a bed is used, target one barely-perceptible level dip entering the outro; detect at composition time. No sequential beat reveals.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none.
- SFX posture: sparse to none. At most a single low, dry tone under the second refrain repeat — no "settle" chime, nothing that resolves.
- Audio-coupled moments: the doubled refrain — the second "only to make themselves happy" should land in silence, the absence of sound doing the work.
- Restraint rule: audio must never swell, warm, resolve, or add rhythm. Silence is the correct default; the words land last and cold.
