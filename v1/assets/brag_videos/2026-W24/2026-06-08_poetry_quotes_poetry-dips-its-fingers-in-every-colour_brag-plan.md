# Brag Plan: "Every Colour Has a Feeling" — the poem where the analyst and the poet collide (Breath of Poetry)

**Chosen hook:** *"10 years measuring the measurable — until a poem mapped a nerve no dataset did."*
**Hook formula:** authority reversal + transformation evidence (the decade-of-measurement authority meets its limit; the poem is the evidence of what changed).
**Tone preset:** polished.
**One-line angle:** A data scientist who spent ten years on things that can be measured watches a poem find the one nerve no dataset mapped — and stops pretending the two lives were ever separate.

## What is this app?
Not an app — a poem (with a short framing essay) titled "Every Colour Has a Feeling," published on **Breath of Poetry**. Its argument is that poetry was already living inside a decade of technical work: "elegant code that reads like a volta," "the commit message / with grief hidden inside it." It waits in the ordinary — burnt toast over the sink — until you are open "just barely." The brag is: real writing, a real published poem, and the honest collision of a measurable career with something no framework covers.

## The angle
The premise is a confession delivered as a piece of writing, not a product demo. The video walks the poem's own turn — from the decade of measurement, into the technical lines where poetry hides (code, a commit message), then the ordinary (burnt toast), and lands on the poem's resolution: the door doesn't need to be wide, "a crack is enough." Specific to this piece, not generic: it uses the poem's actual images and line breaks as the visual beats, and its social proof is the real published title on Breath of Poetry — not a stat, not a "poetry is beautiful" platitude.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The authority-reversal line, restrained, in Cormorant Garamond over the near-black poetry void with slow grain: **"10 years measuring the measurable — until a poem mapped a nerve no dataset did."** The word **nerve** shifts to Cormorant 600 italic as it settles. This is a long line and it IS the hook — it holds the longest of any scene so it lands completely.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The technical hiding place** — the poem's sharpest fusion, one line at a time via weight-shift: *"in elegant code that reads like a volta"* then *"in the commit message / with grief hidden inside it."* This is where the analyst and the poet are provably the same person.
- **The ordinary hiding place** — *"burnt toast over the sink — / the smell specific enough / to be a poem."* The turn from the technical to the domestic; poetry waits in the unremarkable.
- **The proof** — a restrained card with the real published title **"Every Colour Has a Feeling"** and the **Breath of Poetry** wordmark — social proof that the piece landed somewhere real. No URL bar, no fabricated domain.

## Outro / punchline
The poem's closing image, held clean, the resolution phrase carrying the emphasis: **"the door doesn't need to be wide — / a crack is enough."** The word **enough** in Cormorant 600 italic. Hold on near-black, then fade.

## User flow worth showing
none — no app or flow exists. The centerpiece is the poem's own lines plus the published-title proof; the video relies on the Key moments above.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, withdrawn, cinematic-restrained — a poem that found a nerve, treated with reverence, not hype
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, restraint over energy. Pacing is slow; type is large, floating, one line at a time via weight-shift. No chaos, no parody, no bright colour. The subject is a private release, so the video earns attention by conviction and stillness, not by motion.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
Vertical 9:16 for social feeds (Instagram, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn feed) where Breath of Poetry short-form lives. Single-column floating text over grain is built for vertical.

## Duration: ~23s
Extended past the nominal ~19s because the hook line is long (~14 words) and is given the most room to read completely, per the reading-time floor. Poetry lines also want unhurried holds.

Desaturated, withdrawn; analog film grain + vignette throughout; spectral purple only as a faint chromatic edge, never a fill. The real published title on the Breath of Poetry card as proof.

## Share copy (draft)

## Audio direction
- Role: intentional near-silence with a low, withdrawn cinematic bed — the words must land last
- Music: sparse, desaturated ambient drone/piano (low, no melody-forward energy), volume ~0.15. Enter low under the hook, hold flat and quiet through the middle, fade to near-silence under the closing line.
- Music treatment: 1.5s fade in, 2.0s fade out; no swell, no drop, no percussion. Restraint is the point.
- Music cue guidance: to be selected at composition time (no bundled preset chosen here); target ONE faint strongCue near the "a crack is enough" reveal (~19-20s) only if it stays subliminal. Sequential poem lines are NOT beat-snapped — hold each to its read floor, never to a beat grid.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none. No waveform bars, no energy-driven motion — poetry palette is withdrawn.
- SFX posture: none. No "settle" or whoosh effects; this subject rejects them.
- Audio-coupled moments: none of the reveals should feel triggered by sound. If anything, silence widens before "a crack is enough."
- Restraint rule: audio must never become "content" — nothing that turns a quiet poem into a promo.
