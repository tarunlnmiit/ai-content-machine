# Brag Plan: "The Ledger" (leaving home at eighteen, published in ILLUMINATION)

## What is this?
Not an app — a personal essay about the real cost of leaving home young. Ten years of
independence, itemised: the loneliness, the family distance, and the education that made a
small, satisfied life impossible. It was published in the Medium publication **ILLUMINATION**
under its own unchanged headline. The brag is real: real writing, real pickup, live URL.

## The angle
A quiet argument delivered as writing, not a product demo. The video front-loads its sharpest
line as a clean two-line hook — they priced the rent, they never priced the rest — grounds it
in the essay's central ache (independence costs you the version of yourself that could have been
satisfied), turns on the essay's core image (your dream is not a dream, it is an inventory),
proves it ran in a real publication, and closes on the essay's final line. Specific to this
piece: its actual sentences, its own warm photography, and social proof that is a live headline.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The invoice stated flat, as a two-line contrast card over a dimmed train-station photo:
**"They priced the rent and the flights."** (off-white) then **"They never priced the rest."**
(muted). Front-loaded and clean — the contrast IS the hook.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The ache, grounded in the essay's own itemisation: **"Independence does not cost you your
  family. It costs you the version of yourself that could have been satisfied."** over the
  parents photo.
- The essay's core image as an amber Playfair pull-quote (no photo, flat card):
  *"Your dream is not a dream. It is an inventory."* — the argumentative peak.
- The proof: a restrained browser mockup of the live ILLUMINATION article — the real
  (unchanged) headline and the real URL — as social proof that the argument landed somewhere real.

## Outro / punchline
The essay's closing thought, held clean, the key word in sage: **"Their love was the only reason
they could afford it."**

## Tone
- Preset: default (clean, postable; front-loaded contrast pacing)
- Interpretation: fast clean hook, then longer holds on the ache and the proof. Large still
  type, restraint over energy — the subject earns attention by conviction, not motion.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: ~21.0s (reading-time floors extended the ache scene past the 4s storyboard target)

## Visual identity (from the Life niche look bible)
- Background `#1a1208` (warm deep brown), card bg `#231a0e`
- Text `#fdf8f0` (warm off-white), muted labels `#a0856a`
- Accent `#f59e0b` (amber); resolution accent `#86efac` (sage) — reserved for the closing line
- Display font Playfair Display (700 headline, 400 italic pull-quote); body font Inter (400/500/600)
- Warm amber cast + film grain + vignette throughout (the niche's atmospheric constants); the
  essay's own warm photography (train station, parents at the kitchen table) as full-bleed backdrops.

## Audio direction
Warm, sparse intimate bed. Source: local Life-niche BGM ("I Just Want Quiet — Slow Acoustic"),
trimmed from a 155s offset to the exact 21.0s duration, volume 0.20, 1.2s fade in / 1.8s fade
out. Enters low under the hook, holds flat and quiet, fades to near-silence under the closing
line so the words land last. No swell, no drops — restraint is the point.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The invoice (hook) — 4.5s
Dimmed train-station photo. Two-line contrast card: "They priced the rent and the flights."
then "They never priced the rest." Both lines visible together by ~1.5s. Hook must land clean
and fast. Transition: soft crossfade → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — The cost — 6.0s
Parents photo. "Independence does not cost you your family. It costs you the version of yourself
that could have been satisfied." Held to its reading-time floor (the longest line — the
emotional core, given room). Transition: soft crossfade → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — The inventory (pull-quote) — 4.5s
No photo — flat warm radial ground. Amber Playfair italic pull-quote: "Your dream is not a
dream. It is an inventory." Held ~1.8s+. Transition: soft crossfade → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — The proof — 4.0s
Restrained browser-mockup card on warm radial ground: the real (unchanged) headline "Ten Years
Of Independence Taught Me Two Things I Blame My Parents For. I Can Only Write One.", publication
"ILLUMINATION", and the real URL in a URL bar. Factual, no inflation. Transition: soft
crossfade → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — The closing line (outro) — 4.0s
Warm radial ground. Playfair line, key word in sage: "Their love was the only reason they could
afford it." Muted credit line. Fade to warm brown.

**Music mood:** cinematic-restrained (warm, quiet, intimate). The bed enters low, holds flat,
fades under the closing line so the words end on near-silence.
