# Brag Plan: "The Invoice" — Variant A (deadpan) for "Ten Years Of Independence…"

## What is this?
Not an app — a personal essay published in the Medium publication **ILLUMINATION**:
"Ten Years Of Independence Taught Me Two Things I Blame My Parents For. I Can Only Write One."
The essay reads leaving home at eighteen as a bill that came due a decade later — loss stated
as line items, no manufactured resolution. The brag is real writing, really published.
Live URL: `https://medium.com/illumination/ten-years-of-independence-taught-me-two-things-i-blame-my-parents-for-af812b212af0`

## The angle
The video IS an invoice. It reads the essay's own ledger — the cost stated flat, in line items,
withheld of catharsis — exactly as the essay refuses one. This is the deadpan variant: dry
register, cold holds, type-dominant, near-monochrome photography, no swell, no resolution
accent. The proof is a live headline, not a stat.

## Tone
- Preset: deadpan — dry ledger register; loss stated as line items; NO manufactured resolution.
- Interpretation: long cold holds, static (no Ken-Burns push, at most a barely-perceptible drift),
  type dominates, no music swell. The essay refuses false catharsis; the video does too.
- Deliberate creative constraint: **the sage `#86efac` resolution accent is withheld entirely.**
  Amber is the only accent anywhere. There is no green "resolution" beat — because there is no
  resolution.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The essay's opening isolation, stated flat and large over the heavily desaturated train-station
photo: **"I could not remember the last time / I had spoken out loud / to another human being."**
A small muted sublabel lands late: *ten years, itemised.* The line holds long enough to sit.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The pricing line, as two ledger entries: **"He has priced the rent and the flights."** then,
  alone, **"He has not priced the rest."** — a thin amber underline under the second.
- The inventory reframe, type-only: **"Your dream is not a dream. / It is an inventory."**
- The proof: a restrained browser mockup of the live ILLUMINATION article — the real headline,
  the publication name, the real URL in a URL bar. Factual. No inflation.

## Outro / punchline
The essay's invoice line, amber only, held clean, then fade to the canvas brown:
**"Independence does not cost you your family. / It costs you the version of yourself / that
could have been satisfied."**

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
Vertical 9:16 for social feeds where this piece's audience reads.

## Duration: ~24.5s
Five scenes. Longer than the reference brag because deadpan wants long cold holds, and the outro
line (18 words) needs its full reading floor before it can exit.

## Storyboard (crossfades 0.5s, tracks alternate 1/2/1/2/1)
- **S1 hook (0 – 6.0, t1):** train-station photo, heavily desaturated/dimmed. Three-line isolation
  quote lands and settles ~4.5s. Muted sublabel *ten years, itemised.* late.
- **S2 (5.5 – 10.5, t2):** parents photo, very dim. Line 1: "He has priced the rent and the
  flights." Line 2 lands alone: "He has not priced the rest." with a thin amber underline.
- **S3 (10.0 – 14.5, t1):** type-only flat card. "Your dream is not a dream. / It is an inventory."
- **S4 proof (14.0 – 18.5, t2):** restrained browser-mockup card — real headline + "ILLUMINATION"
  + real URL in a URL bar. Factual, no inflated language.
- **S5 outro (18.0 – 24.5, t1):** flat field. The invoice line — AMBER ONLY, no sage. Fade to
  the canvas brown at the very end.

## Visual identity (from the Life niche look bible)
- Background `#1a1208` (warm deep brown), card bg `#231a0e`
- Text `#fdf8f0` (warm off-white), muted labels `#a0856a`
- Accent `#f59e0b` (amber) — the ONLY accent used. Sage `#86efac` deliberately absent.
- Display font: Playfair Display (700 headlines, 400 italic pull-quotes)
- Body font: Inter (400/500/600)
- Photography desaturated to near-monochrome; amber cast dropped in favour of a cold, dim
  treatment; film grain + vignette throughout (the niche's atmospheric constants).

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, sparse, LOW cold-ledger bed — kept lower than the reference (~0.18 volume).
  Near-silence is acceptable; nothing swells.
- Source: `v1/assets/audio/bgm/life/itYCLFXGQMc_[No Copyright Music] I Just Want Quiet - Slow Acou.mp3`
- Treatment: trim to 24.5s, `volume=0.18, afade in 1.5s, afade out st=22.5 d=2.0`. Flat under
  the whole piece; no beat-grid, no swell, no drop. The words land last.

## Share copy (draft)
Ten years of independence, itemised. I never priced the rest — the loneliness, the version of me
that could have stayed small and been satisfied. ILLUMINATION published the invoice.
