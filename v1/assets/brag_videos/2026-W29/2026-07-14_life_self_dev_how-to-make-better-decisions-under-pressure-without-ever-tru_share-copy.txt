Stop trusting your gut — under pressure it reads the size of the number, not the shape of the work.

My gut loved a candidate. Sharp in the room, great energy, every instinct said hire him. Then the scored rubric graded him a 61 against a hard cutoff of 75 — a ₹20-lakh mistake it caught while my instinct was walking me straight into it.

My gut was measuring charisma. The sheet was measuring whether they could do the job.

So here's the reframe that replaced instinct for me: don't ask what a decision feels like. Ask what you'd have to see to know you're wrong — and go look.
