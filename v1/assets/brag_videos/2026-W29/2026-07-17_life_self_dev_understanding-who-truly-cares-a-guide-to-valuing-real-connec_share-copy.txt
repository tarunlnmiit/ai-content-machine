I read data for a living and had never once run the numbers on my own friendships.

41 messages from me. 9 back. 6 of them just thumbs-ups.

I spent six months auditing one person's silence and zero minutes noticing the friend who drove forty minutes to drop off a charger — the one who showed up for everything without ever being asked.

Not being someone's priority is information about their priorities. Not a verdict on your worth.
