# Brag Plan: "The Forty Percent" — deadpan brag for the mental-health essay

## What is this?
Not an app — a personal essay published in the Medium publication **An Injustice!**:
retitled headline "The Silent Killer No One Talks About (And It's Not What You Think)".
Original working title: "The Lie We Inherited About Strength." The essay is a personal,
self-incriminating confession about male mental-health silence: a data-minded person
admitting he white-knuckled life for years — passive-aggressive in meetings, awake at 3am
running loops he couldn't switch off — because he'd inherited the lie that strength requires
silence. The brag is real writing, really published. Live URL exists:
`https://aninjusticemag.com/the-silent-killer-no-one-talks-about-and-its-not-what-you-think-4680ca8fd155`.

## The angle
The video IS the confession. It reads the essay's own interior voice — a quiet, flat
admission delivered slow, with long holds and minimal motion. This is the deadpan variant:
dry register, cold holds, type-dominant, near-monochrome photography, no swell. The proof is
a restrained browser mockup showing the real headline, publication, and URL — factual, no
inflation.

## Tone
- Preset: deadpan — slow, low-energy, personal confession; the essay's actual interior voice.
- Interpretation: long cold holds, static (at most a barely-perceptible drift), type
  dominates, no music swell. Photos held at reduced opacity so the words are the subject.
- Colour discipline: amber `#f59e0b` is the single kinetic accent (on "40%"). Sage `#86efac`
  is reserved EXCLUSIVELY for the final outro line — the only resolution colour in the video.

## Hook (first 5 seconds)
Over a dimmed window photo, stated flat and large: **"40% of men won't talk about this."**
A smaller, muted line lands below: *I was one of them.* The line holds long enough to sit.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The confession, one flat admission over the same dimmed window photo: **"I white-knuckled
  life. Passive-aggressive in meetings. Awake at 3am running loops I couldn't switch off."**
- The pull-quote, Playfair italic over a near-black / dimmed conversation photo: **"I told
  myself that was the cost of ambition. It wasn't."**

## Proof
A restrained browser-mockup card: URL bar with the real URL, the real headline "The Silent
Killer No One Talks About (And It's Not What You Think)", and the publication "An Injustice!".
Factual — no inflation, no invented metrics.

## Outro / punchline
The essay's load-bearing line, held clean in sage: **"Vulnerability isn't the opposite of
strength. It's proof of it."** Fade to canvas brown.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
Vertical 9:16 for social feeds.

## Duration: ~20.2s
Five scenes. Deadpan wants long cold holds; the confession sentence (15 words) sets the
reading floor. Duration follows the reading floor.

## Storyboard (crossfades ~0.6s, tracks alternate 1/2/1/2/1)
- **S1 hook (0 – 5.0, t1):** window photo, dimmed. "40% of men won't talk about this." then
  muted "I was one of them."
- **S2 (4.4 – 10.0, t2):** window photo continues, dimmed. The confession, one flat line.
- **S3 (9.4 – 13.6, t1):** conversation photo, dimmed near-black. Playfair-italic pull-quote.
- **S4 proof (13.0 – 16.6, t2):** dark field. Restrained browser-mockup card — URL bar, real
  headline, publication "An Injustice!". Factual.
- **S5 outro (16.0 – 20.2, t1):** dark field. The closing line in sage (the only sage in the
  film). Fade out.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, sparse, LOW cold bed. Near-silence is acceptable; nothing swells.
- Source: `v1/assets/audio/bgm/life/itYCLFXGQMc_[No Copyright Music] I Just Want Quiet - Slow
  Acou.mp3`, trimmed from a ~110s source offset (fresh, distinct from sibling builds).
- Treatment: 20.2s, `volume=0.18, afade in 1.5s, afade out st=18.2 d=2.0`. Flat under the
  whole piece; no beat-grid, no swell. The words land last.
