# Brag Plan: "The One Skill That Makes You Good at AI" (Medium essay)

**Chosen hook:** "The one skill behind being good at AI has nothing to do with AI."
**Hook formula:** Curiosity gap + Contrarian (both assigned formulas fused — the line
opens an information void *and* contradicts the assumed answer of "the tool").
**Tone preset:** polished
**Angle (one line):** A data scientist who writes novels shows that the same move —
asking a precise question, not owning a secret tool — powers code and fiction alike, and
the proof is one prompt structure that visibly works on both.

---

## What is this app?
Not an app — a personal/technical essay arguing that the skill behind using AI well is the
same skill behind good data science: asking a precise, well-structured question. The author is
a ten-year data scientist who also writes novels, and the essay shows three real, copy-paste
prompts (fiction, code, a hard day) that all share one anatomy: role + specific ask +
constraints + room for your judgment. The brag: real writing, real craft, a genuinely useful
idea — no tool-shilling.

## The angle
The premise is a quiet reveal, not a product demo. The video walks the essay's own turn: the
assumed answer ("you need a better tool") is wrong; the real answer is the *question*. It proves
this the way the essay does — by showing the shared skeleton of two prompts that live on opposite
sides of the brain (a novel twist-generator and a senior-engineer debug prompt) and letting the
viewer see the same four-part structure underneath both. Specific to this piece, not generic: it
uses the essay's actual prompts as the centerpiece visual and its social proof is the live Medium
headline, not a stat.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The contrarian curiosity line at full scale, restrained, in Inter ExtraBold on the deep-navy
canvas: **"The one skill behind being good at AI has nothing to do with AI."** Given the most
room of any scene so the whole claim reads and the void opens before the payoff.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The reframe** — the essay's thesis as a clean line: *"It's not the tool. It's the question."*
  This is the argumentative pivot the hook promised.
- **The proof (centerpiece)** — the two real prompts shown as code/terminal cards side by side or
  in sequence: the novel twist-generator ("Generate 8 escalating 'what if' twists… rank them by
  how much they'd hook a reader") and the debug prompt ("You are a pragmatic senior engineer…
  give the smallest correct fix"). Then the four shared parts highlight in on both: **Role ·
  Specific ask · Constraints · Your judgment.** Same skeleton, two worlds.
- **The proof it landed** — a restrained browser mockup of the live Medium article: the real
  headline and the `medium.com/@tarun-gupta` byline, as social proof this is real published writing.

## Outro / punchline
The essay's closing turn, held clean, the resolution phrase in accent cyan `#06b6d4`:
**"The analyst and the writer were always the same person. AI just got them talking."**

## User flow worth showing
none — no app or flow exists. Centerpiece is the two real prompts (as code cards) plus the
four-part structure reveal and the published-headline proof.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: serious, elegant, technical-but-warm — a good idea that earns attention
  by conviction, not noise
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, restraint over energy. Reveals are slow and
  deliberate; the code cards are the star and get room to be read; no chaos, no hype, no bullet
  spray. The four-part highlight arrives one part at a time, held.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920

## Duration: ~22s
Slightly over the nominal ~19s because the hook is a long sentence (~12 words) and the code-card
proof scene needs enough settled time to actually read two prompts — both are given room per the
reading-time floor.

## Visual identity (from the DS niche look bible)
- Background: `#0a0e1a` (deep navy canvas) / card bg `#111827`, glass `rgba(17,24,39,0.85)`
- Accent: `#3b82f6` (blue — emphasis / the "question" word) and `#06b6d4` (cyan — code + the
  closing resolution phrase); `#8b5cf6` (purple) reserved for the subtle structure-highlight sweep
- Text: `#f0f4ff` (near-white); muted labels `#64748b`
- Display font: Inter 800 (hook / big lines)
- Body font: Inter 500/600; code in `JetBrains Mono` 400
- Strongest visual element: the essay's two real prompts rendered as clean code/terminal cards on
  the navy canvas, with the four shared parts (Role · Specific ask · Constraints · Your judgment)
  highlighting in accent color. Subtle grain/vignette; disciplined, premium restraint.

## Share copy (draft)
Everyone thinks being good at AI is about the tool. It's not — it's the question. Same prompt
skeleton I use to debug code, I use to write novels. Wrote it up on Medium.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents over a low, warm-but-clean bed — restrained, tech-editorial,
  no drop, no percussion pump
- Music: a slow, minimal ambient/electronic bed (calm, confident). Enter low under the hook, hold
  flat and quiet through the reveal, fade to near-silence under the closing line so the words land last.
- Music treatment: no swell, no beat drop; volume posture is a floor, not a build. Restraint is
  the point for polished.
- Music cue guidance: track TBD from bundled library; if a preset exists, target 1 soft strong-cue
  at the code-card reveal (Scene 3) and 1 at the closing line. Otherwise detect at composition time.
  Beat-grid window only for the four-part structure reveal (see Scene 3) — hold each part to its
  read floor, not on a fast beat.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none-to-subtle; at most let the code-card presence breathe faintly with
  the bed. No waveform bars.
- SFX posture: sparse. At most a single soft key-tick cluster if the prompt text types in, and one
  quiet settle on each of the four structure parts. Professional restraint — no whooshes.
- Audio-coupled moments: the prompt text appearing (optional light key ticks); the four parts
  arriving one by one (a quiet tick per part).
- Restraint rule: audio must never turn this into a hype reel. No drop, no big swell, no dense SFX.
