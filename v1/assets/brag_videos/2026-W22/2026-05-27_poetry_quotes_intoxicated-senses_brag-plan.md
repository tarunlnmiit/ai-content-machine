# Brag Plan: "Intoxicated Senses" (Poetry)

**Chosen hook:** "A real hangover ends by morning. There's no morning after you."
**Hook formula:** cold open + contradiction (states the rule of a hangover, then breaks it)
**Tone preset:** cinematic
**Angle (one line):** Love arrives like alcohol and never sobers up — walk the sensory catalog of the beloved to the collision of heart and mind, and land on the fact that this hangover has no endpoint.

## What is this piece?
Not an app — a poem. "Intoxicated Senses" reads love as intoxication: booze wears off, this doesn't. It catalogs the beloved (twinkling eyes, enlightening smile, ebony hair), then turns inward as heart and mind — once in sync — collide and refuse to comply, leaving the speaker "without a ground to stand on or a sky to cover my head with." The brag is real writing with a real published home on Breath of Poetry.

## The angle
The hangover metaphor works because a hangover has an endpoint — and this one doesn't. The video walks the poem's own arc: it opens inside the contradiction (a hangover ends; you don't), moves through the poem's actual sensory images and comparisons, hits the heart-vs-mind collision, and lands on the closing image of losing both ground and sky. Specific to THIS poem, not generic "poetry is beautiful": every on-screen line is a real line from the text, and the proof is the piece's own published home, not a stat.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The contradiction stated in full, restrained, over a dark cinematic field: **"A real hangover ends by morning. There's no morning after you."** Two beats — the rule, then the break. This holds the longest of any scene so the turn lands completely. Large Cormorant display over near-black with a slow amber ember glow.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The intoxication that won't leave** — the poem's core image: *"the effects of booze reside for a short period — but your intoxication doesn't want to go away."* The heart enjoying the hangover.
- **The sensory catalog** — the beloved rendered in the poem's own images, revealed one by one: *"your twinkling eyes / your enlightening smile / your ebony hair."* Stuck "like a newborn sticks to her mother."
- **The collision** — the argumentative peak: *"my mind and heart worked in sync — but their ecosystems have collided, deciding not to comply with each other."*

## Outro / punchline
The poem's closing image, held clean: **"leaving me without a ground to stand on, or a sky to cover my head with."** Then the poem's title and published home as quiet proof.

## User flow worth showing
none — no app or flow exists. Centerpiece is the poem's own lines rendered as full-bleed cinematic type, plus the published-home proof.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: intimate, trailer-scale restraint — a private intoxication treated at blockbuster scale but never loud
- Interpretation: 4-5 scenes, 3-5s each. Big type, full-bleed fields, dramatic slow reveals rather than quick cuts. Each line lands before the next begins. The subject is longing, not spectacle — the drama comes from scale and hold, not from motion density or effects.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: ~22s
Slightly extended because the hook is a two-clause contradiction (~12 words) and is given the most room to read completely, per the reading-time floor.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support — a low, warm, intimate bed with a single slow swell, not a dense score
- Music: slow cinematic ambient / warm piano-and-drone mood; enter under the hook, one gentle swell into the collision scene, settle to near-silence under the closing line so the last words land last
- Music treatment: start low under the hook, hold flat through the sensory catalog, allow one restrained swell as the heart/mind collision hits, then fade toward silence under the outro. No drop, no percussion, no beat-drop energy — restraint is the point.
- Music cue guidance: track TBD (cinematic-ambient, ~70-80 BPM feel). Target one strong-cue timestamp for the collision reveal (~Scene 4, to be detected at composition time). Beat-grid window only for the 3-line sensory catalog in Scene 3 — hold each line to its read floor, do not snap tight to the beat.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let the ember glow breathe with the low-end RMS; do not add waveform bars or visible meters.
- SFX posture: sparse. At most one soft low "settle"/impact under the collision line and a faint air/room tone. No clicks, no whooshes on every transition.
- Audio-coupled moments: the 3-line sensory catalog (each line's arrival gets the faintest tonal lift); the collision line (soft low impact + swell).
- Restraint rule: audio must never turn this into a hype reel. No beat drop, no riser-into-bass-hit, no percussive cuts. Silence under the closing line is intentional.
