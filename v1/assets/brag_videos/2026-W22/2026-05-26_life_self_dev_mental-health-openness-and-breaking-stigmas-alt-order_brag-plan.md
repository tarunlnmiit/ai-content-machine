# Brag Plan: "The Order" (cinematic) — for "The Lie We Inherited About Strength"

## What is this?
Not an app — a personal essay on mental-health stigma and the cost of telling men to
"man up." Titled in draft "The Lie We Inherited About Strength," it was published, retitled
by the editor, in the Medium publication **An Injustice!** as
**"The Silent Killer No One Talks About (And It's Not What You Think)"**
(live: aninjusticemag.com/the-silent-killer-no-one-talks-about-and-its-not-what-you-think-4680ca8fd155).
The brag is: real writing, real pickup, on a subject that costs lives.

## The angle
A quiet argument delivered as a piece of writing — an order given and then refused. The video
opens on the instruction we hand every boy ("man up") and immediately names its price. It walks
the essay's spine: the suicide gap, the insult buried inside "vulnerability," and lands on the
reconciling line that turns the whole thing over — vulnerability isn't the opposite of strength,
it's proof of it. This is the *cinematic* variant: the drama lives in the light and the slow
push-ins, not the pace. One real photo — two men on a bench — bookends the film: it opens on
them and closes back on them, dimmer.

## Hook (first 4 seconds)
The bench photo, full-bleed and warm, slow push-in (scale 1.0→1.06). Two lines land fast:
**"Stop telling him to man up."** then **"It's killing him."** — "killing him" in warm-red
`#ef4444`, the one and only warm-red in the whole film. The hook must land clean and quick.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The stat, over the window photo: *"Globally, men die by suicide at far higher rates than
  women. That's not statistical noise."* Amber lands on **statistical noise**.
- The turn, over the conversation photo — a Playfair italic pull-quote:
  *"Vulnerability gets called weakness so often men start believing the insult. It's exactly
  backwards."*
- The proof: a restrained browser mockup of the live **An Injustice!** article — the real,
  editor-chosen headline and the real URL — social proof that the writing landed somewhere real.

## Outro / punchline (the bookend)
Return to the bench, dimmed further than the hook. The essay's reconciling line lands in
Playfair Display with exactly one sage accent in the whole film, on **strength** →
**"Vulnerability isn't the opposite of strength. It's proof of it."** A quiet hold, a small
muted credit — *AN INJUSTICE! · 2026* — then a fade to warm brown.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: urgent, elevated stakes, warm push-ins — a plea that got recognized
- Interpretation: five scenes, warm full-bleed light, slow push-ins, calm holds. The urgency
  is in the stakes (a killer, an order that hurts), not in fast cutting. One real photo opens
  and closes the film as a deliberate bookend.

## Format & duration
Vertical 9:16 — 1080x1920. Target ~20.5s.

## Visual identity (Life niche look bible)
- Background `#1a1208` (warm deep brown), card bg `#231a0e`
- Text `#fdf8f0` (warm off-white), muted labels `#a0856a`
- Accent amber `#f59e0b` (emphasis words); warm-red `#ef4444` used exactly ONCE, on "killing him"
- Resolution accent sage `#86efac` — used exactly ONCE, on "strength" in the outro
- Display: Playfair Display (700 headline, 400 italic pull-quote). Body: Inter (400/500/600)
- Warm amber cast + film grain + vignette throughout (Life niche atmospheric constants)

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, intimate acoustic bed (I Just Want Quiet — slow acoustic)
- Arc: enters low under the hook, holds quiet through the middle, a gentle lift at the proof
  reveal, then fades under the closing line so the words land last
- Treatment: source offset ~95s (fresh window), trimmed + faded with ffmpeg to the exact
  video duration (20.5s): volume 0.24, 1.5s fade-in, 2.0s fade-out from 18.5s
