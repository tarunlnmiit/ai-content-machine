# Brag Plan: "The Room No One Entered" (poem)

**Chosen hook:** *"There's a version of me still mid-sentence in a room no one entered."*
**Hook formula:** hyper-specificity + pattern interrupt (the poem's own devastating image; the "room no one entered" reframe IS the break)
**Tone preset:** freeform: **cinematic-restrained** (built-in `cinematic`, dialed down — quiet devastation, long holds, minimal SFX)
**Angle (one line):** the quiet horror of competence — passing the salt, knowing whose mug is whose, being *useful, in place* — is the exact shape of being gone, and no one can name what's missing.

## What is this?
Not an app — a poem about the particular loneliness of being reliably present while feeling absent
from your own life. A person who is very good at showing up (passes the salt before it's asked for,
knows which mug is whose, arrives on time) and who, in the soap and steam of an 11pm sink, has
forgotten what he wanted to say ten years ago. Published on Medium as **"The Room No One Entered."**

## The angle
The premise is quiet devastation, not spectacle. The video walks the poem's own turn: it opens on the
frictionless competence — the small domestic gestures anyone would call "fine" — and lets that
competence curdle into the reveal that being *present like furniture is present* is a way of being
gone. The horror is that nothing is dramatic. Nobody worries. The days clock in and out without him.
Specific to this poem, not generic "poetry is beautiful": every on-screen line is an actual line
from the poem, and the emotional engine is the gap between how capable he looks and how absent he is.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The poem's sharpest image, held long: **"There's a version of me / still mid-sentence / in a room
no one entered."** Large serif over a dim, dust-lit empty room (the essay's own photo of empty chairs
in fading light). It lands slow and holds — this is the longest single read in the video, and the
"room no one entered" is both hyper-specific and the pattern break that earns the next 20 seconds.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The competence, stated as evidence, one plain line at a time:
  *"I pass the salt before they ask. / I know which mug is whose."* — small, exact, unremarkable.
- The vanishing at the sink, the poem's quietest gut-punch:
  *"and somewhere in the soap and steam / I forget what I wanted / to say ten years ago."*
- The reframe — competence named as absence, the argumentative peak:
  *"being present / like furniture is present, / useful, in place."*

## Outro / punchline
The poem's closing devastation, held clean, then a slow fade to dark:
**"not missing anything / anyone can name."** After it settles, the title **"The Room No One Entered"**
appears small beneath — the piece's real headline as quiet proof it landed somewhere real.

## User flow worth showing
none — no app or flow exists. The centerpiece is the poem's own lines and imagery, carried by
restraint. No product recreation.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic (dialed down to **cinematic-restrained**)
- Creative direction: quiet devastation — long holds, minimal motion, no swell, no spectacle
- Interpretation: fewer beats, each held past comfort. Pacing is slow and even; type is large and
  still. Cinematic scale in the type and full-bleed frames, but every dramatic instinct is pulled
  back — the poem earns attention by conviction and stillness, not by cuts or energy. Motion is a
  slow settle, never a slam.

## Share copy (draft)
I got so good at showing up that I disappeared from my own life. A poem about being present like
furniture is present — useful, in place, not missing anything anyone can name. "The Room No One Entered."

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic-restrained bed — a low, warm sustain with a single almost-imperceptible swell under
  the hook, then flat and quiet through the middle. No percussion, no drop.
- Music: slow, sparse, intimate instrumental (warm piano or held strings, low in the mix). Enter under
  the hook, hold flat and quiet through the domestic lines, fade toward near-silence under the closing
  line so "not missing anything anyone can name" lands in the quiet.
- Music treatment: fade-in ~0.8s under the hook; one barely-there swell as the hook settles; flat hold
  through the middle; slow fade to near-silence over the final 3s.
- Music cue guidance: track to be selected at composition time (candidate mood: "I Just Want Quiet" or
  similar slow acoustic bed). Beat sync is NOT wanted — this is a rubato, holds-driven piece; ignore
  the beat grid and time reveals to the reading floor, not to tempo. One strong-cue target only: a
  single low swell aligned to the hook settling (~2s). Detect at composition time if a preset exists.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none-to-subtle. At most, let the vignette/grain breathe very slightly with
  the low sustain. No waveform bars, no reactive text.
- SFX posture: sparse to none. Optionally one soft, distant water/sink texture under the "soap and
  steam" line if it can be near-subliminal; otherwise silence. No "settle" or "whoosh" cues.
- Audio-coupled moments: the hook's swell settling; the near-silence under the closing line.
- Restraint rule: audio must never build to a climax, never add a beat-synced reveal, never punctuate
  a line with a sound effect. The silence at the end is the loudest thing in the piece.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The room (hook) — 6.5s
Full-bleed photo of empty chairs in fading dusty light, dimmed under warm amber cast + grain + heavy
vignette. Three-line Playfair Display image lands slowly, one line settling after the next, then holds
together: **"There's a version of me / still mid-sentence / in a room no one entered."** Given the most
room of any scene so it reads completely. Motion is a slow fade-up and the faintest drift, never a slam.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the three lines settle one after another (~0.7s apart), then the full
three-line block holds ~3.5s. Slow, legible, not beat-timed.
Audio intent: a low warm bed fades in; a single almost-imperceptible swell as the block settles.
Audio-coupled idea: the swell aligned to the hook settling — no SFX.
Music: slow, sparse, intimate — very low in the mix.
Transition mood: slow crossfade (0.8s) → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The competence — 4.5s
The room photo drifts darker / slightly out of focus behind. Two plain Inter lines arrive one at a
time and hold, deliberately unremarkable: *"I pass the salt before they ask."* then *"I know which mug
is whose."* Flat delivery — these are the "everything is fine" evidence before the turn.
Sequential/interaction: yes — line one holds to its floor (~1.6s settled), then line two appears and
holds (~1.6s). No beat sync; each line gets its full read.
Audio intent: bed holds flat and quiet — nothing responds, which makes the calm feel wrong.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: unchanged, flat.
Transition mood: slow crossfade (0.8s) → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The sink — 4.5s
Near-empty warm brown canvas, dimmed, faint grain — the room recedes so the words carry alone. Playfair
italic, held: *"and somewhere in the soap and steam / I forget what I wanted / to say ten years ago."*
"ten years ago" in dim amber `#f59e0b`. This is the quiet gut-punch; hold well past comfort.
Sequential/interaction: none — the line appears as one slow fade and holds to its full read floor
(~3.5s settled). Stillness is the point.
Audio intent: quietest moment; the bed nearly recedes. Optional near-subliminal water texture, or
silence.
Audio-coupled idea: at most a distant, barely-there sink texture — only if it stays subliminal.
Music: fading slightly toward the floor.
Transition mood: slow crossfade (0.8s) → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Furniture (the reframe) — 4s
Empty warm canvas, almost black at the edges. Playfair Display, the argumentative peak:
**"being present / like furniture is present, / useful, in place."** "furniture" in dim amber. This is
where competence is named as absence. Hold to its full read.
Sequential/interaction: none — one slow reveal, held.
Audio intent: the bed holds low and unresolved — no swell, no lift, refusing to release the tension.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: low, flat, quiet.
Transition mood: slow crossfade (1.0s) → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Not missing anything (outro) — 4.5s
Warm brown fading to near-black. Playfair Display, the closing devastation, held clean:
**"not missing anything / anyone can name."** Let it settle into the quiet, then the music fades to
near-silence. After the line holds ~2.5s, the title **"The Room No One Entered"** appears small in
muted `#a0856a` beneath it — the poem's real headline as quiet proof. Slow fade to near-black.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the closing line holds alone first (~2.5s), then the small title fades in
beneath and holds (~1.5s) before the fade-out. Restraint, not a reveal beat.
Audio intent: music recedes to near-silence so the words land last; the silence is the loudest thing.
Audio-coupled idea: the near-silence under the closing line.
Music: slow fade to near-silence over the final ~3s.
Transition mood: slow crossfade to near-black; end on stillness.
