# Brag Plan: "The Wake-Up Ritual" (Life & Self-Development essay)

**Hook line:** "The first thing I do every morning is put my face in a bowl of ice water."
**Hook formula:** hyper-specificity + pattern interrupt (the essay's literal opening line — one weirdly specific lived moment that also breaks the expected coffee/phone morning)
**Tone preset:** cinematic
**Angle (one line):** Treat a quiet three-part morning like a trailer for a discipline film — open on the ice-water plunge, walk the ritual built to survive a real married life, and land on the reframe that returning, not streaks, is the win.

---

## What is this app?
Not an app — a personal essay on doing the same three-step morning for two years: ice-water face plunge, journaling, meditation. The pitch isn't productivity; it's a "wake-up ritual" engineered to survive a shared life (a spouse, evenings worth protecting, workdays you don't control) instead of the 5am grind. The brag: real writing, a real two-year practice, published under the Life niche.

## The angle
The video plays a quiet self-care essay as if it were a cinematic trailer — big declarative type, dramatic reveals, restraint. It walks the essay's own turn: the shocking-specific opening image (face in ice), the pattern interrupt (not coffee, not the phone), the three steps stated like a creed, and the reframe that gives the piece its spine — "morning routine" out, "wake-up ritual" in; streaks are not the goal, returning is. Specific to this essay because it uses its actual opening line, its actual three named steps, and its actual closing philosophy — not generic morning-routine b-roll clichés.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Full-bleed cold blue-grey frame. Large display line lands and holds: **"The first thing I do every morning is put my face in a bowl of ice water."** This IS the hook — hyper-specific, sound-off legible, and it sets up the pattern interrupt that Scene 2 pays off. Longest single hold of the video so the sentence reads completely.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The pattern interrupt** — three quick denials land, then the point: *"Not coffee. Not my phone. Not a gratitude journal I abandoned by February."* Breaks the expected morning-routine open.
- **The three steps as a creed** — Ice bath. Journaling. Meditation. — revealed one by one, each with its one-line role ("the reset switch" / "clearing the decks" / "the anchor"). This is the ritual, stated like superpowers.
- **The reframe (argumentative peak)** — the essay's sharpest turn as a full-bleed pull line: *"I don't have a morning routine. I have a wake-up ritual."* followed by the philosophy beat: *"Streaks are not the goal. Returning is."*

## Outro / punchline
The essay's stripped-down closing requirement, held clean, then the title. **"Not 5am. Not two hours. A bowl of cold water, a notebook, and silence."** → title card: **THE WAKE-UP RITUAL** with the essay's subtitle line "A morning that survives a real life." Music swell implied, then settle.

## User flow worth showing
none — no app or flow exists. Centerpiece is the essay's own language: the opening image, the three named steps revealed in sequence, and the reframe. Rely on Key moments.

Energy comes from scale, contrast, and a low musical swell — never from clutter or speed. Restraint is the point: this is a calm ritual, sold with conviction.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support — a low, cold ambient bed with one restrained swell into the reframe, settling under the outro.
- Music: slow cinematic/ambient bed (cold, spacious; not upbeat, not parody). Pick a Life-niche cinematic track; enter low under the hook.
- Music treatment: enter quiet under Scene 1, hold flat and spacious through the three steps, allow one gentle swell as the reframe lands (Scene 4), then fade toward near-silence under the closing line so "silence" reads literally.
- Music cue guidance: track + tempo to be confirmed at composition time. Target 1 strong swell cue at the reframe line ("I have a wake-up ritual"). Beat-grid window for the three step-names in Scene 3 — reveal them on alternating beats, each held to its read floor, never snapped faster than ~0.9s apart. Restraint note: cinematic-restrained, not a hype drop.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let the cold frame's grain/vignette or a faint water ripple breathe with the bass; no waveform bars.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, professional restraint. One optional cold water plunge/whoosh under the hook reveal; one soft settle under the outro title. Nothing else.
- Audio-coupled moments: the three step-names arriving one by one (soft tick/settle per name); the single swell on the reframe.
- Restraint rule: no percussive hype, no rapid stingers, no comedic SFX. This is a calm ritual film — the audio must stay dignified and let the closing "silence" land quiet.
