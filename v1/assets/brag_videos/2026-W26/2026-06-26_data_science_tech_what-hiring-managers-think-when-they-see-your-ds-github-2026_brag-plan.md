# Brag Plan: "What hiring managers think when they see your DS GitHub in 2026"

**Chosen hook line:** "Nobody lost their job to AI. They lost it to a senior who learned to use one."
**Hook formula:** Contrarian + Authority reversal (the essay's own thesis line; a hiring manager reversing the "AI took our jobs" narrative)
**Tone preset:** freeform — **wry-contrarian** (dry wit, needling authority, deadpan-adjacent but more pointed); fallback base preset: `deadpan`
**Angle (one line):** A ten-year hiring manager's flat verdict — the DS job market didn't close because AI took the work; it closed because a folder of `.ipynb` files stopped being a portfolio and became a confession.

---

## What is this?
Not an app — a data-science career essay written by a ten-year practitioner who also hires. Its argument: nobody was replaced by AI; senior practitioners using AI erased the *need* for the junior role, and a GitHub full of exploratory notebooks now reads as half a job done. The brag is real writing making a sharp, uncomfortable call the field keeps avoiding.

## The angle
The video plays the hiring manager straight — dry, unbothered, slightly merciless — and lets the essay's own reversals do the cutting. It opens on the thesis line, shows the literal artifact being judged (a folder of `.ipynb` files getting flagged), needles the comfortable excuse ("AI took the jobs"), lands the essay's wittiest line ("a directionless AI is a very confident random walk"), and closes on the flat instruction the whole piece builds to. Specific to this essay: it uses the real notebook filenames, the real "red flag" verdict, and the essay's actual sharpest sentences — not generic career-advice motion graphics.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black canvas. Inter 800: **"Nobody lost their job to AI."** lands hard and holds — then, beneath it, **"They lost it to a senior who learned to use one."** fades in. This is the essay's opening line and the whole thesis; it gets the most room so both halves read completely. No motion flourish — the flatness is the wit.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The red flag.** A code-editor file listing types in, JetBrains Mono: `eda.ipynb`, `features.ipynb`, `model.ipynb`, `README.md`. A dry red label stamps over it — **"Red flag."** Caption turn: *"In 2019, this was a portfolio. In 2026, it's a confession."*
- **Who actually got replaced.** Deadpan reframe: *"AI didn't replace junior data scientists."* → *"It deleted the need for them."* (The essay's real claim: senior + AI erased the role, not the person.)
- **The wit peak.** The essay's sharpest line, full display: **"A directionless AI is a very confident random walk."** — "very confident random walk" in accent cyan.

## Outro / punchline
Restrained Medium article mockup — the real headline and author byline as proof the piece is live — then the flat closing instruction the essay ends on: **"Put it on GitHub as a codebase. Not a notebook."** Cut clean.

## User flow worth showing
none — no app or flow exists. Centerpiece is the essay's argument plus one recreated artifact (the flagged notebook folder) and the published-headline proof.

## Tone
- Preset: `deadpan` (base) — driven toward **wry-contrarian**
- Creative direction: a hiring manager stating uncomfortable truths without raising their voice; dry, pointed, needling, never a hype reel
- Interpretation: few scenes, long holds, big empty space, one thought at a time. The needle comes from *content* (reversals, the red-flag stamp), not from pace or volume. Restraint makes the verdict land harder.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: ~20s
Extended slightly past nominal because the hook sentence is long (~16 words) and is the whole thesis — it gets the most room to read completely, per the reading-time floor.

- Background: `#0a0e1a` (deep navy-black) / card + editor bg `#111827`

- Text: `#f0f4ff` (near-white); muted labels / filenames `#64748b`

- Strongest visual element: the recreated file-explorer listing of `.ipynb` files getting a red "Red flag." stamp — the essay's abstract claim made literal and video-worthy.

## Share copy (draft)

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse, dry, professional — a low minimal bed, not a hype track. The tone is flat on purpose.
- Music: low ambient/minimal electronic bed at reduced volume; enter under the hook, hold flat, fade under the closing line so the last words land in near-silence. `none` only if unavailable.
- Music treatment: no swell, no drop, no percussive build. Volume posture stays low and even throughout — restraint is the joke.
- Music cue guidance: pick a minimal/low-key bundled bed at composition time; target one soft cue for the "Red flag." stamp (~scene 2) and let the close fade to quiet. No beat-grid syncing of text — holds are too long for beat reveals.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none. Keep it flat.
- SFX posture: sparse, dry, motion-matched. One dry mono keypress/tick as each filename types in; one flat, non-dramatic thud when "Red flag." stamps. Nothing else.
- Audio-coupled moments: the filename listing (typed, with dry ticks); the red-flag stamp (single flat thud).
- Restraint rule: audio must never turn this into a hype/energy reel. No risers, no whooshes on text, no swell on the outro. Quiet and dry only.
