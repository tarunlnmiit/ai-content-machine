# Brag Plan: "The ML Skill Bootcamps Won't Teach You" (Data Science essay)

**Chosen hook:** "94% on training. 71% on test. They were ready to ship it."
**Hook formula:** Negativity bias + Hyper-specificity (loss/dread framing carried on two weirdly specific numbers)
**Tone preset:** freeform — *ominous-warning* (deadpan base, cinematic escalation)
**Angle (one line):** A data scientist generated a churn model in three prompts, saw 94% accuracy, and was ready to ship it — never seeing the 71% test score that meant it would die in production. The skill the AI bootcamps skip is the one that catches what the AI builds wrong — and it's the one that keeps you employable.

## What is this app?
Not an app — a Data Science / Tech tutorial-essay (Tutorial 6 of 10) arguing that agentic AI is creating a generation of practitioners who can *generate* ML models but can't *read* them. Its spine is a true story: a churn model built entirely through Claude, 94% train / 71% test, a textbook 23-point overfitting gap the builder couldn't see. The brag is the essay's own argument made visceral: the number on the screen can lie to you, and knowing that is the skill that survives automation.

## The angle
The video walks the essay's own turn — from the two numbers that look like success and disaster side by side, to *why* the gap happened (no train/test split), to the essay's sharpest line about being "just skilled enough" to be fooled, and lands on the reframe: this readable-output skill is what keeps a data scientist employable while everything around it gets automated. Specific to this essay, not generic — it uses the actual 94/71 numbers, the actual missing line of code, and the essay's real quotable. No fabricated "published in X" proof scene (this slug is not confirmed live in the submissions tracker; honesty guardrail).

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
**"94% on training."** lands first (accent blue, big Inter Black number), then **"71% on test."** drops beneath it in code-red — the gap made visual. The dread line follows on the same canvas: **"They were ready to ship it."** Two numbers, one impending disaster, fully sound-off legible.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The gap named:** the two numbers connected by a **"23-point gap"** callout, with the quiet verdict *"Textbook overfitting. It would fail in production within a week."* Escalating dread, stated flat.
- **The cause, in code:** a JetBrains Mono block showing the model trained and evaluated on the same data — the missing `train_test_split` — the one skipped step that produced the gap. The product's real material: the code the AI wrote that no one could read.
- **The quotable (argumentative peak):** *"The most dangerous place to be is just skilled enough to use the tools — without being skilled enough to know when the tools are lying to you."*

## Outro / punchline
The dread converts to the payoff: **"Reading a model's output is the skill that survives while the others get automated."** then the tighter landing — **"It's also the one that keeps you employable."** Held clean on the dark canvas.

## Tone
- Preset: freeform *ominous-warning* — fallback base **deadpan** (calm, long holds, one thought at a time, understatement makes the dread land), with a **cinematic** escalation arc across scenes (the warning grows heavier, then resolves into the payoff).
- Creative direction: dry, matter-of-fact statements of risk; the calm delivery is what makes the warning ominous. Never loud, never chaotic — chaos would fight the dread.
- Interpretation: 4 scenes, long settled holds, big sparse type, minimal motion. Numbers and code carry the frame. Energy comes from tonal escalation and hard reveals, not from speed. The outro must land the payoff so the "brag" is *why this essay matters*, not just doom.

## Audio direction
- Role: low, ominous, sparse bed — a quiet sustained tension tone, not a beat-driven track. The dread lives in restraint.
- Music: dark ambient / low drone, entering under the hook and holding flat; a subtle low swell into the quotable (Scene 3), then easing under the payoff so the final line lands in near-silence.
- Music treatment: enter low, hold, one restrained swell at the argumentative peak, fade under the outro. No drop, no percussion energy.
- Music cue guidance: track to be selected at composition time (no bundled preset assumed). Target 1 strong-cue timestamp for the "71%" drop in Scene 1 and 1 for the quotable reveal in Scene 3; both to be detected at composition time. No sequential beat-grid needed (holds are long and deliberate).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — allow the dark canvas / number glow to breathe faintly with the low bed; no waveform bars.
- SFX posture: sparse. One dry low "drop" cue as **71%** appears (the disaster lands); one soft keystroke tick set if the code block types in. Nothing more.
- Audio-coupled moments: the **71%** reveal (dry drop), the code block if it types in (subtle key ticks), the payoff line's arrival.
- Restraint rule: audio must never get hype or upbeat — this is a warning, delivered calm. No build-drop EDM energy, no celebratory sting on the payoff.
